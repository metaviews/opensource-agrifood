## Problem addressed

Community land trusts need incorporation documents. The UK's national CLT Network publishes model rules — a Registered Society form under the Co-operative and Community Benefit Societies Act 2014, with a share issue option — drafted by Anthony Collins Solicitors, as sector infrastructure to save every new CLT from drafting from scratch.

## Open layer

None — explicitly negated. The document's own copyright notice (verified from the downloaded PDF, 2026-11-18):

> "These model rules are copyright to and the intellectual property of the National CLT Network. They may not be photocopied or otherwise reproduced either in part or in full without the express written permission of the National CLT Network, for which a fee may be charged."

## What is actually open

The model rules are **free to download and read** (verified: direct PDF, no paywall), alongside an Incorporation Resource Pack with a table of legal structures for community-led housing/land groups. The rules are complete, adoption-ready documents — 2,400+ lines of text — the exact artefact an open licensing layer would want to fork.

## Governance and control

The National CLT Network (a registered charity) controls; solicitors (Anthony Collins — Rose Klemperer, David Alcock) drafted. Even the movement's own national body fences its template: the model rules function as a membership-service asset, and the licence reserves a fee-based permission regime.

## Evidence of use

UK CLTs incorporate under these model rules (the model rules cite registered society forms; individual CLT registrations visible on the FCA mutuals register use "MODEL RULES" language). Adoption depth not re-verified this pass.

## Maintenance and funding

Charity; rules versioned (2025 edition checked).

## Why it belongs

It extends the C3 licensing-gap finding to its logical extreme and to the UK: **even the national body whose mission is multiplying CLTs forbids photocopying its own template.** Free-to-read model legal documents with explicit no-reproduction terms — in the movement whose entire purpose is replication. For the succession program, this is the international proof that the gap is not regional immaturity but a structural default in how the sector prices its legal knowledge.

## What it does not demonstrate

That the Network refuses permission in practice (the clause reserves "a fee may be charged" — enforcement practice unverified); that no other UK legal format is openly licensed; farm-specific applicability (UK CLTs are housing-majority, though the rules are generic community-benefit society rules usable for land-holding bodies).
