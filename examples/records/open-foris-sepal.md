- Last checked: 2026-08-14

## Problem addressed

Agroforestry, silvopasture, and perennial tree-crop systems require complex 3D canopy estimation, land-cover classification, and carbon stock verification across fragmented landscapes. Traditional annual farm software cannot model tree canopy dynamics, while commercial satellite MRV (Measurement, Reporting, and Verification) platforms charge high proprietary subscription fees that lock out Global South forest stewards and smallholder agroforestry cooperatives.

## Open layer

Comprehensive suite of open-source software under permissive MIT and Apache licenses:
- `SEPAL` (System for Earth Observation Data Access, Processing and Analysis for Land Monitoring): MIT License, cloud-based platform connecting Google Earth Engine, optical/radar satellite imagery, and Python/R analysis workflows.
- `Open Foris Collect` / `Collect Earth` / `Arena`: MIT License, field survey design and high-resolution spatial sampling tools.
- `Whisp` (Forest Data Partnership): Open tools for tracing deforestation-free commodity supply chains (EUDR compliance).

## What is actually open

- Code: Repository `openforis/sepal`, 237 stars (MIT; active commits Aug 2026) and `openforis/collect` (MIT; 59 stars).
- Documentation: Freely available training manuals, video modules, and documentation hosted by FAO.
- Data Integration: Zero-cost access to Landsat, Sentinel-1/2, NICFI high-res tropical basemaps, and planetary scale cloud computing.

## Governance and control

Developed and maintained by the Forestry Division of the Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (UN FAO) in collaboration with international donors (Norway's NICFI, Finland, Germany).

## Evidence of use

- Active in over 180 countries with tens of thousands of registered users spanning national forest inventory teams, indigenous land monitoring programs, and regional agroforestry initiatives.
- Primary technical engine used by tropical nations to calculate REDD+ forest carbon baselines and smallholder agroforestry tree canopy density.

## Maintenance and funding

Sustainably funded via multilateral UN donor programs, international climate funds, and UN FAO operational budgets.

## Why it belongs

Open Foris and SEPAL are the planetary gold standard for open-source tree-crop, canopy, and forest-agriculture monitoring. They provide the open scientific and computational substrate that allows perennial agroforestry systems to participate in climate verification, deforestation monitoring, and canopy carbon modeling without proprietary SaaS barriers.

## What it does not demonstrate

SEPAL is a regional/landscape-scale earth observation and field inventory platform; it does not generate micro-scale orchard row-planting CAD files or automated tractor keyline paths for single-tree planting operations.
