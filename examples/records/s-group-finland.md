# S-ryhmä / SOK (S Group Finland)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: Finland — "S Group consists of 19 independent regional cooperatives and SOK, which is owned by the cooperatives" (s-ryhma.fi); ~49% self-reported grocery market share; ~2.5 million members (~78% of Finnish households, verified as reported)
- Project: customer-owned grocery retail federation (Prisma, S-market, Sale, ABC, S-pankki) — the dominant grocery retailer in Finland operating under consumer-cooperative ownership
- Field-guide context: opened via the grocery concept's Finnish co-op ecosystem survey (`grocery/research/finnish-co-op-survey.md`, 2026-11-25); load-bearing quotes re-verified at source same day
- Record type: governance contrast record — curated with absence as the finding, the Finnish mass-scale counterpart to the Arctic Co-operatives record

## Problem addressed

None in the conventional sense — S-ryhmä is not an alternative seeking viability; it is the incumbent. The record exists for the question the corpus keeps asking of co-op ownership: what does ownership structure produce at full market dominance, when co-op retail *wins* rather than struggles?

## The open layer — and the honest finding

**Ownership-open, everything-else-closed — co-op ownership as the incumbent's form.**

- **Governance (verified)**: 19 independent regional cooperatives own SOK, the central company; customer-owners are the member-owners of the regional osuuskaupat (s-ryhma.fi).
- **Scale (verified, self-reported)**: grocery market share 47% (2022) → 48.8% (2024) → 49 (2025), published on S-ryhmä's investor page citing the Nielsen Grocery Shop Directory. Nielsen's own tables were not accessed; the figure is self-reported.
- **Membership (verified as reported)**: "S Group has about 2.5 million members—in a country of just 5.6 million inhabitants—representing 78 percent of Finnish households" (The American Prospect, 2023). Denominators conflict across sources (2.7M customer-owners on s-ryhma.fi's 2025 page; ~4M S-Etukortti card holders per kuluttaja.fi) — members, card holders and household share are different counts, recorded unreconciled.
- **The scale story (verified)**: "going from about 16 percent of the Finnish grocery market in the early 1990s to 47 percent today" and "the cooperative structure was consolidated and simplified, from 202 regional co-ops in the early 1980s to just 19 today" (Prospect) — co-op form maintained *through* concentration.
- **Data and systems layer: closed, with search evidence.** No open data, open datasets, or public APIs were located for S-ryhmä/SOK (searched: ownership/market-share sources, loyalty-data journalism, developer-facing surfaces; nothing open surfaced). What the public knows about its customer-data layer comes from privacy journalism: a 2013 Yle Kuningaskuluttaja register-access investigation found "Keskolla on kanta-asiakkaistaan enemmän tietoa kuin S-ryhmässä" (Kesko holds more profiled data than S-ryhmä) and that S-Etukortti purchases "tallentuvat tietokantoihin, mutta niissä ei ole tunnistetietoja" (stored in databases without identifying details) — 2013-era findings; current app-era practices were not examined.

## Why it belongs

1. It is the collection's scale counterweight: every other large co-op retail story in the corpus is contraction or defection (FCL losing Calgary Co-op; Co-op Atlantic defunct). S-ryhmä shows co-op ownership reaching near-total market dominance — and staying co-op while doing it.
2. It sharpens the corpus's core negative finding at its strongest test: if any grocery operation's co-op ownership might plausibly produce open information infrastructure, it is one owned by ~2.5 million of its own customers — and none was found. Ownership-open without systems-open, at the largest scale available.
3. Its consolidation history (202→19 regional co-ops) documents the mechanism by which co-op federations reach scale — evidence the concept's federation design work needs.

## What it does not demonstrate

- Any open artifact — there is none; the record is curated with absence as the finding.
- That co-op ownership *caused* market dominance (consolidation, the bonus system, and Finnish market structure are entangled causes).
- Current data practice — the privacy findings are 2013 journalism, pre-app-era; S-mobiili and present-day S-Etukortti handling were not examined.
- Data-privacy superiority over Kesko as a durable fact — it is one 2013 register-access comparison, recorded as such.
