# Ekylibre

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: France-origin / global; open-source farm management information system (FMIS)
- Project: https://github.com/ekylibre/ekylibre ; site: https://ekylibre.com/ (timeout on 2026-08-03 check; repository is the primary evidence)
- Field-guide context: none

## Problem addressed

Farm management software locked to vendors, with data siloed per platform. Ekylibre provides an open-source FMIS ("Connecting farms to the world") for farm records, planning, and operations, so farms can run management software without vendor lock-in.

## Open layer

Open software: full FMIS application, AGPL-3.0, with the source code as the core asset. Not open data and not open hardware.

## What is actually open

Verified 2026-08-03 via GitHub API: ekylibre/ekylibre, AGPL-3.0 (spdx verified), created 2011, 487 stars, 170 forks, not archived, pushed 2026-07-26 — an active open-source FMIS with a long maintenance record (15 years). Documented in the academic literature as a canonical French open-source agriculture case (Chance & Meyer 2017, "L'agriculture libre", Techniques & Culture 67, cited 24, analyzing Ekylibre alongside L'Atelier Paysan as two distinct materializations of open source in agriculture).

## Governance and control

Company-backed open source: the project is developed by the Ekylibre company (open-core framing per Chance & Meyer 2017 — the company develops the software and offers paid services around it). The open layer is the code; service revenue is the maintenance model.

## Evidence of use

487 stars / 170 forks and 15 years of continuous pushes indicate an active project and community. Deployment-scale evidence (number of farms running self-hosted Ekylibre) was not verified in this pass.

## Maintenance and funding

Company-maintained (open core + paid services); active pushes through 2026-07-26. One of the collection's clearest instances of the hybrid open-core + paid-services archetype, alongside farmOS and ODK.

## Why it belongs

Ekylibre is the active, licensed, French-origin open-source FMIS the collection's open-software layer was missing; it also anchors the French-language academic current on open source in agriculture.

## What it does not demonstrate

Ekylibre does not demonstrate open data, open hardware, or farmer-controlled governance of the platform: it is company-backed open software with a service model, and its deployment scale is unverified.
