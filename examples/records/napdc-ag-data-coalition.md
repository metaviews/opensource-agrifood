# NAPDC / Ag Data Coalition (Growers Agricultural Data Cooperative)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: United States (national)
- Project: https://www.agdatacoop.org/ ; Ag Data Coalition: https://agdatacoalition.org/
- Field-guide context: new discovery from the data-cooperatives cycle, 2026-08-02

## Problem addressed

US farmers' data is stored, shared, and monetised through vendor platforms that often claim perpetual rights. The Grower Information Services Cooperative (GiSC), founded by grower Billy Tiller, was described in 2017 as "the only grower-owned data cooperative in the United States": a grower-formed data warehouse and sharing cooperative governed by a board of directors composed of the growers' peers. It merged its platform with the Agricultural Data Coalition (ADC), a nonprofit formed by 14 founding members (including AGCO, CNH Industrial, the American Farm Bureau Federation, and universities), to create AgXchange — a neutral, secure, grower-controlled data repository. The lineage continues as the National Agricultural Producers Data Cooperative (NAPDC).

## Open layer

Farmer-controlled data governance and cooperative storage: growers control, store, view, and share their data assets with researchers and service providers at their own discretion.

## What is actually open

- The governance arrangement is the open layer: a grower-owned cooperative with peer governance, neutral storage, and explicit grower-permissioned sharing (including with universities for noncommercial research).
- Current activity: NAPDC is co-creating a farmer-centered Agricultural Data Governance Framework with the National Policy Consensus Center (Portland State University); the workgroup launched May 2025, meets monthly, and published draft "NAPDC Ag Data Principles" (December 2025) with meeting summaries through 2026. The project is led by the University of Nebraska-Lincoln with NIFA Open Data Framework (ODF) funding.
- The underlying platform software's openness is not documented in the sources read; the openness here is governance, not code.

## Governance and control

Grower-controlled: cooperative governance by growers' peers (GiSC/GADC); the ADC nonprofit contributed the university/industry coalition. Current NAPDC governance is producer-led with university coordination. Farmers hold the control position by design; the framework work is explicitly about defining roles, responsibilities, and shared principles.

## Evidence of use

- 2017 primary source (Hoosier Ag Today) documents the GiSC-GADC-ADC merger, the AgXchange platform, the grower-board governance, and the founding-member coalition.
- Current NAPDC site documents the active governance-framework work (meeting minutes May 2025-January 2026, draft principles, annual meeting presentation).
- NIFA project pages document the cooperative's strategic development under the Open Data Framework programme.
- Membership scale, platform usage, and whether the cooperative achieved durable commercial operation were not verified in this pass.

## Maintenance and funding

University-led coordination (UNL) with NIFA ODF funding and neutral-facilitation support (NPCC); the cooperative model's long-term funding and member base are the open question.

## What this case demonstrates

NAPDC/ADC is the clearest US example of the data-cooperative governance form the project's G-OSA-07 question asks about: grower-owned, peer-governed, consent-based data storage and sharing, built specifically against vendor perpetual-rights practices. Its current governance-framework co-design is active, which is rare for this category.

## What it does not demonstrate

- It does not establish durable operation at scale; membership and platform usage are unverified.
- The platform software's openness (source, licences) is unverified.
- The framework is co-design in progress (principles drafted December 2025), not a settled governance outcome.
- Cooperative economics beyond grant funding are unverified.
