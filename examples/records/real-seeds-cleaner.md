## Problem addressed

Cleaning, winnowing, and separating viable seeds from chaff, weed seeds, and hollow hulls is one of the most labor-intensive bottlenecks in small-scale seed production, varietal preservation, and community seed banking. Commercial industrial seed cleaners (fanning mills, gravity separators) cost thousands of dollars and are sized for large grain operations, while traditional manual hand-winnowing is imprecise, weather-dependent, and slow.

## Open layer

Open hardware design plans and construction manuals published freely online under an open-access public design license. The machine uses a vacuum cleaner/blower, clear acrylic tubing, and standard PVC plumbing fittings to create a precision vertical air-column separator (zig-zag / aspirator column).

## What is actually open

- Design & Build Plans: Fully illustrated step-by-step DIY plans, cutting dimensions, materials lists, and operation guides published openly on `realseeds.co.uk`.
- Replicability: Designed specifically with commodity, off-the-shelf parts (plastic tubes, plywood, standard domestic vacuum source).
- Open Commons Ethos: Published by Ben Gabel and Kate McEvoy (Real Seeds) explicitly to foster non-hybrid open-pollinated seed independence.

## Governance and control

Maintained and documented by Real Seeds (an independent UK heirloom and open-pollinated seed company founded in 2000). The design has been gifted to the global seed sovereignty commons without patent restrictions or commercial licensing fees.

## Evidence of use

- Widespread independent replication across hundreds of small seed companies, university agroecology research farms, NGOs, and community seed libraries across the UK, Europe, North America, and Australia (e.g., Hudson Valley Seed Co., Southeastern Grasslands Initiative, Koanga Institute).
- Adapted by Farm Hack and regional seed alliances as the benchmark open-source seed aspirator.
- High physical durability and operational efficiency: can clean 10–20 kg of small-to-medium seed per hour with calibrated vacuum airflow control.

## Maintenance and funding

Self-funded by Real Seeds through their commercial open-pollinated seed catalogue. The plans are hosted permanently as an educational public good.

## Why it belongs

The Real Seeds Seed Cleaner is a textbook example of open-source post-harvest appropriate technology. It directly enables community seed sovereignty (linking directly to OSSI, Bioleft, and SEARICE) by removing the physical equipment bottleneck in seed processing through an accessible, highly verified open-hardware design.

## What it does not demonstrate

It is an air-separation aspirator for small-to-medium seeds (brassicas, tomatoes, beans, flowers); it is not an industrial multi-screen fanning mill for high-tonnage combine-harvested grain processing.
