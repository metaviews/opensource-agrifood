# Kulturland eG (Germany)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: Germany (national cooperative; farm-transfer focus)
- Project: https://www.kulturland.de/hofuebergabe
- Live docs: model description + published cooperative statute (Satzung)
- Field-guide context: the C8 pass's most complete community-capital succession structure — a cooperative whose members fund farm transfers with interest-free capital
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

A farm transferring to a non-family successor needs capital nobody can raise at
farmland prices. Kulturland eG's model: members contribute interest-free long-term
capital (verified verbatim: "Genossenschaftsmitglieder stellen hierfür zinsloses und
langfristiges Kapital ohne Gewinnbeteiligung zur Verfügung"), the cooperative buys the
farm, and leases it long-term to the successor farmer — converting transfer events into
community land ownership.

## Open layer

Free-to-read, detailed model documentation — including the **published cooperative
statute** and the full structure (eG cooperative + KG + gemeinnützige Stiftung for
buildings, tax treatment of below-market sale, Erbbaurecht purpose-binding). No open
licence: the structure is replicable as knowledge, the documents are not forkable.

## What is actually open

- The full Hofübergabe (farm transfer) model description with tax and foundation
  mechanics.
- The cooperative statute as a published document.
- Scale and operating evidence via the cooperative's own reporting (not re-verified
  this pass).

## Governance and control

Registered cooperative (eG); member-controlled; no profit participation — the capital
is patient by constitution, not by contract.

## Evidence of use

Operating registered cooperative with transfer cases documented on its own pages
(counts not re-verified this pass).

## Maintenance and funding

Member capital; cooperative operating revenue.

## Why it belongs

It answers the design document's P5 question structurally: what does a
**non-family transfer shelter** look like when built as a community-capital vehicle
rather than a tax measure? Kulturland's interest-free, profit-free member capital is
the purest patient-capital form found — and its published statute is the closest thing
to a template the community-capital cell produced. For Canada, the import question is
the cooperative-securities pathway (community bonds already exist in the corpus:
ZooShare, Coop Agri-Énergie Warwick's bonding).

## What it does not demonstrate

An open licence on the statute or model; Canadian cooperative-law applicability;
verified transfer counts; that interest-free capital scales beyond a committed member
base.
