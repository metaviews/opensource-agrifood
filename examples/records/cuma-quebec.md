- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: Canada (Québec; the operating machinery-co-op layer)

- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

Machinery is a major entry-capital line a beginning farmer cannot carry alone.
CUMAs (cooperatives d'utilisation de matériel agricole) let member enterprises share
equipment use, lowering production costs without each farm owning the machine.

## Open layer

The MAPAQ portrait document states its own reuse terms (verified from the PDF text
layer): "**La reproduction totale ou partielle du présent document est autorisée à la
condition que la source soit mentionnée**" — full or partial reproduction permitted
with attribution. The most permissive licence found on any Canadian government
succession-adjacent document in this program (cf. OMAFRA Pub 70's Crown copyright).

## What is actually open (verified from the PDF, 2026-11-18)

- Scale: "**Actuellement, on compte 64 CUMA en activité au Québec**" with **2,025
  member enterprises** (2021 data).
- The purpose frame: "La CUMA permet d'améliorer l'efficacité technico-économique
  des entreprises agricoles et de **réduire les coûts de production** en favorisant
  l'utilisation, à moindre coût, d'équipements performants."

## Governance and control

Member cooperatives (farm-enterprise members); MAPAQ documents the sector. The
machinery-sharing savings literature (Harris & Fulton, Saskatchewan, via Iowa State
Extension: $44.66→$28.75/acre, ~36% reduction, dated) supplies the economics; the
CUMA network supplies the operating proof.

## Evidence of use

64 operating CUMAs with 2,025 member enterprises is the largest verified
machinery-sharing deployment in Canada.

## Maintenance and funding

Member-funded cooperatives; MAPAQ documentation and support.

## Why it belongs

C4's shared-capital cell needed a Canadian operating model rather than a dated study,
and the CUMA layer is exactly that — with a government document that is actually
licensed for reuse, making it the one piece of Canadian succession-adjacent state
material this program has found that could legally be forked into the adaptation
commons. It also bounds the open-stack question from the non-ownership side:
sharing achieves ~36% machinery-cost reductions without any open technology at all.

## What it does not demonstrate

Entrant-specific uptake (CUMAs serve established enterprises too); transferability to
other provinces' cooperative statutes; current-year economics (2021 counts).
