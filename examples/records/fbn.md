# Farmers Business Network (FBN)

- Status: `candidate` (contested "farmer-owned" platform)
- Region / reach: United States (national; expanding internationally)
- Project: https://www.fbn.com/
- Field-guide context: new discovery from the data-cooperatives cycle, 2026-08-02

## Problem addressed

FBN presents itself as a farmer-to-farmer network that pools farm data anonymously to give growers benchmarking, input procurement leverage, and market transparency against what it frames as an unfair ag market.

## Open layer

None documented. The network's data pooling and "farmer-first" framing are the contested surface; the company is venture-capital-owned, and its data-handling terms are contractual, not open.

## What is actually open

- No open source, open data, or open governance located. The relevant analytical content is the gap between the "farmer-owned network" framing and the ownership reality: founded 2014 by Amol Deshpande (a Kleiner Perkins partner); raised US$917.9 million across 9 rounds; investors include Google Ventures, Temasek, T. Rowe Price, Fidelity, and others.
- Data pooling is described as anonymous, with security and confidentiality claims; the company states it does not share member data.

## Governance and control

Company-owned, venture-capital-backed, with an IPO trajectory (Forge Global documents the IPO timeline and financing details). Farmers are members whose pooled data feeds the platform; control sits with the company and its investors.

## Evidence of use

- Reported reach: FBN visited by 35% of surveyed farmers when shopping for inputs (Stratus Ag Research survey, company-cited).
- HBS case study documents the crowdsourced-data model; AgFunder and AWS coverage document the data-pooling mechanics.
- The data-safety question is live: "How safe is farmers' data if FBN goes public?" (Future Farming).

## Maintenance and funding

VC-funded ($917.9M raised); IPO as the projected exit. The tension between member data and shareholder value is the contested core.

## What this case demonstrates

FBN is the reference example of the "farmer-owned" framing applied to a venture-backed platform: the strongest available case for why the project's asset-level discipline matters — ownership claims must be checked against governance, funding, and data terms, not branding.

## What it does not demonstrate

- It does not establish that pooled data benefits farmers as framed; the 35% survey figure is company-cited.
- Data-handling terms were not audited in this pass (candidate status).
- It is not a cooperative, commons, or open platform in any documented sense.
