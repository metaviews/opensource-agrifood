# Stock Synthesis 3 (SS3)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: international (NOAA origin; adopted by DFO Pacific in 2021)
- Project: https://github.com/nmfs-ost/ss3-source-code
- Repository: https://github.com/nmfs-ost/ss3-source-code
- Field-guide context: new discovery via the fisheries/aquaculture sectoral scan (G-OSA-34, sub-cell 1); companion record to sdmTMB; verified 2026-09-04

## Problem addressed

Age- and size-structured integrated stock assessment — the core computational engine fisheries managers use to set quotas and assess stock status. SS3 is NOAA Fisheries' flagship platform and one of the world's most widely used assessment engines.

## Open layer

Open-source software under the most frictionless licence in the corpus's software records.

## What is actually open

**CC0 1.0 Universal**, verified verbatim from the repository's LICENSE file (GitHub API spdx_id CC0-1.0). Maintained by NOAA's National Marine Fisheries Service Office of Science and Technology (nmfs-ost). Actively maintained: pushed 2026-09-02, 45 stars.

## Governance and control

NOAA NMFS OST stewardship with an international user/developer community; documentation site at nmfs-ost.github.io/ss3-website.

## Evidence of use — including Canada

DFO's Pacific Biological Station (Nanaimo, BC) **adopted SS3 in 2021** for BC groundfish assessments (Offshore Rockfish Program, replacing Awatea/Coleraine), documented in DFO Canadian Technical Report of Fisheries and Aquatic Sciences 3806 (Haigh, Starr, Fisch & Edwards, 2026), which notes SS3 "was considered expedient to transfer... cited by all users of SS3 at PBS." SS3 is therefore not just NOAA software that Canada happens to read — it is the assessment engine Canada's Pacific groundfish science runs on.

## Why it belongs

SS3 is the zero-licence-boundary extreme of the fisheries cell's finding: the sector's core scientific infrastructure is public code under a public-domain dedication, maintained by a national science agency, and adopted across borders without negotiation. Companion to sdmTMB: together they show a state science ecosystem (NOAA + DFO) whose assessment layer is fully open while its operational data layer is fully closed — the split that defines the cell.

## What it does not demonstrate

Any open layer in fisheries *operations* — SS3 consumes confidential catch and composition data behind closed doors; the openness is of the engine, not the inputs. No Canadian code contributions to SS3 were verified (adoption is as a user).
