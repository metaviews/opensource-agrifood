# Farm Credit Canada and Farm Management Canada — the transition-tool layer

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: Canada (national)
- Project: FCC transition hub: https://www.fcc-fac.ca/en/knowledge/transition ; FMC Farm Transition Toolkit: https://fmc-gac.com/farm-transition-guide/ (farmtransitionguide.ca)
- Live docs: FCC Terms of Use: https://www.fcc-fac.ca/en/online-policy/terms-of-use
- Field-guide context: the C1 pass's lender-authored closed layer — the country's most-used transition tools, authored by its largest farm lender, under terms that forbid forking
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

Canadian farmers encountering succession planning meet two national portals first: FCC's transition knowledge hub (estate-planning worksheets, "Pathway to Transition Checklist", a will and estate pre-planning quiz) and Farm Management Canada's Farm Transition Toolkit. Both are free. Neither is open — and both carry the same structural feature: FCC, a federal Crown-corporation farm **lender**, is the author of one and a funder/partner of the other.

## Open layer

- **FCC: explicitly closed.** Its Terms of Use (verified verbatim 2026-11-18): "you are entitled to copy, download and print any content for your own personal use but you may not otherwise, copy, modify … redistribute, republish or reproduce, in whole or in part, any such content … or create any derivative works based on such content, without FCC's prior written consent."
- **FMC: unlicensed.** The Farm Transition Toolkit site states no licence anywhere (verified 2026-11-18); its framing is "Over the next 10 years, 75% farms will change hands, yet only 8% of farmers have a written transition plan" (verified verbatim — FMC's own figures, recorded as disagreement with StatCan's 12.0% coverage and the CBA's 88% no-plan figure, not averaged).

## What is actually open

Nothing in this layer. The worksheets and checklists are downloadable but sit under the terms above; the FMC toolkit's licence status is simply unstated.

## Governance and control

FCC: a federal Crown corporation and the country's dominant agricultural lender — its planning content comes from a party with a lending interest in the transition. FMC: a national nonprofit whose toolkit was built with industry partners including FCC (FCC confirmed as partner/funder on the page checked). The lender relationship is disclosed but the planning tools are not independent of it.

## Evidence of use

FCC is the largest agricultural lender in Canada; its checklist and worksheets are among the most-circulated transition tools nationally. FMC is the national farm-business-management umbrella body. Usage metrics not verified this pass.

## Maintenance and funding

FCC: Crown-corporation funded. FMC: nonprofit; toolkit partners include FCC and BDO (per its project page).

## Why it belongs

It records the demand-side reality the succession program's open layer must displace: when a Canadian farm family searches for succession tools, they find free, useful, professionally produced instruments that are (a) authored or funded by the sector's dominant lender and (b) legally unforkable. An open Canadian succession layer is therefore also a **lender-independence layer** — the design constraint is recorded here so the concept document and any future vehicle carry it.

## What it does not demonstrate

That FCC's terms are enforced against non-commercial educational adaptation (enforcement practice unverified); that the FMC toolkit's licence is closed (it is unstated — a who-to-ask item); usage metrics.
