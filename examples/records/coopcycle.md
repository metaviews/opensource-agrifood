# CoopCycle

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: European Union & International (France-origin / 70+ delivery co-ops across Europe and the Americas)
- Project: https://coopcycle.org / https://github.com/coopcycle
- Live docs: https://coopcycle.org/en/coopyleft/
- Field-guide context: addresses the short-chain agricultural distribution, last-mile food logistics, and cooperative delivery layer — an open-source logistics and marketplace platform restricted to worker-owned delivery cooperatives
- Last checked: 2026-08-14

## Problem addressed

Delivery gig-economy platforms (Deliveroo, UberEats, DoorDash, Instacart) extract 20–35% commissions from local restaurants, bakeries, and farm produce hubs while misclassifying couriers as precarious gig workers. Direct-to-consumer farm-to-table hubs and urban food cooperatives need dispatch routing, real-time tracking, and multi-vendor marketplace software without submitting to extractive venture capital delivery platforms.

## Open layer

Source-available with strong cooperative copyleft ("Coopyleft"). The codebase is publicly available on GitHub (`coopcycle/coopcycle-web`), but commercial use is legally restricted exclusively to worker-owned cooperatives that employ their couriers and adhere to International Co-operative Alliance (ICA) governance principles.

## What is actually open

- Code: Repository `coopcycle/coopcycle-web`, 600 stars (checked 2026-08-14), actively maintained with daily commits (Aug 2026). Complete Symfony/React stack for dispatch, geocoding, order checkout, and mobile apps (React Native).
- The Coopyleft License: A novel legal instrument that requires any commercial entity using the software to be democratically controlled by workers and offer formal employment contracts. Non-commercial and research use is unconstrained.

## Governance and control

Governed as a European cooperative federation (CoopCycle European Cooperative Society / SCE). Member cooperatives vote democratically on software roadmaps and collective bargaining standards.

## Evidence of use

- Deployed across 70+ federated delivery cooperatives in over 10 countries (France, Spain, UK, Germany, Canada, Argentina, Mexico).
- Powers local food hubs, CSA vegetable box deliveries, artisan bakery distribution, and prepared-meal delivery.

## Maintenance and funding

Funded through monthly cooperative federation dues, public solidarity grants (e.g. European social innovation programs), and contributions from member co-ops.

## Why it belongs

CoopCycle represents the forefront of *platform cooperativism* applied to food and agricultural logistics. It introduces the "Coopyleft" licensing innovation to explicitly prevent platform enclosure by corporate delivery monopolies, demonstrating how software licensing can be architected to defend worker and producer governance.

## What it does not demonstrate

Because of the Coopyleft clause, CoopCycle is not OSI-certified open source (it restricts commercial use to a specific governance form); it is not a drop-in white-label delivery app for conventional private logistics corporations.
