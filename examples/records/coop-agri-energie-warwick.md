# Coop Agri-Énergie Warwick & Canadian agricultural energy cooperatives

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: Canada — Warwick, Centre-du-Québec (`Coop Agri-Énergie Warwick`); Toronto, Ontario (`ZooShare Biogas Co-operative`); Chatham-Kent / Southwestern Ontario (`AGRIS Solar Co-operative`)
- Project: https://www.agrienergie.coop/ · https://coopcarbone.coop/en/flagship-projects/coop-agri-energie-warwick/ · https://zooshare.ca/ · https://www.agrissolarcoop.ca/
- Field-guide context: opened via the C4 energy and biological byproducts scan (G-OSA-40, `research/2026-10-energy-biological-byproducts-scan.md` sub-cell 4); governance record — like IGFC (`igfc-mobile-slaughter.md`), Terre de Liens (`terre-de-liens.md`), and Arctic Co-operatives (`arctic-cooperatives.md`), curated for what cooperative ownership accomplishes at the capital/grid wall and what it leaves closed; verified 2026-10-14

## Problem addressed

Individual small and mid-sized farms cannot cross the capital, regulatory, and utility-interconnection thresholds of modern energy infrastructure: upgrading raw biogas to pipeline-grade renewable natural gas (RNG) requires $10M+ upgrading skids and utility off-take contracts, while rural solar PV hits distribution-transformer capacity caps and prime-agricultural-land restrictions. Cooperative aggregation allows farmers and community bondholders to pool feedstock, capital, and transformer access.

## Open layer — and the honest finding

**Governance-open (cooperative ownership and community-bond financing), systems- and data-closed.** Across all three flagship Canadian agricultural/bio-energy cooperatives verified for this record, the cooperative structure solves the capital and interconnection threshold, while the plant engineering, control software, and operating data remain turnkey commercial and unlicensed:

1. **Coop Agri-Énergie Warwick (Warwick, Centre-du-Québec — Canada's first agricultural producer RNG cooperative)**:
   - **Ownership & governance**: Founded by **12 dairy farms and a local cheese producer** in MRC d'Arthabaska in partnership with **Coop Carbone** (a Quebec solidarity cooperative that developed and operates the complex), with a board composed primarily of the member agricultural producers (launched 2019-12-06; operational 2021).
   - **Scale & economics**: **$12M capital project** supported by $3M from Quebec's Technoclimat program and a $1.7M repayable contribution from Canada Economic Development (CED). Processes **50,000 tonnes/year** of livestock manure and regional organic residues into **2.3 million m³/year (~83,500 GJ/yr per CBA 2026 Appendix B)** of biomethane injected into the **Énergir** natural-gas network under a **20-year off-take contract**, avoiding ~6,500 tonnes CO₂e/yr and returning digestate fertilizer to the member farms.
   - **Technology & openness**: Digesters were built commercially by **Dominion & Grimm** and gas purification/upgrading by **Air Science**; no engineering designs, PLC control software, operating datasets, or cooperative governance templates are openly licensed.
2. **ZooShare Biogas Co-operative Inc. (Toronto, Ontario — Canada's first community-bond biogas co-op)**:
   - **Ownership & governance**: Non-profit renewable energy cooperative founded 2011; raised **>$5M in community bonds from 800+ members** and structured a Limited Partnership with Oshawa Power to build and operate the plant at the Toronto Zoo (commercial operation began 2021-04-01; Amended REA No. 4501-9VJK3N issued 2025-04-22).
   - **Scale & technology**: Processes **17,000 tonnes/year** (2,000 t Toronto Zoo animal manure + 15,000 t GTA grocery/food waste) in a commercial **Fitec Self-Cleaning digester** to generate **500 kW** for the Toronto Hydro grid under a 20-year Ontario FIT2 contract expiring 2038.
   - **Openness**: Community-bond financing model and school curriculum are shared publicly; plant hardware, control stack, and operational telemetry are proprietary.
3. **AGRIS Solar Co-operative (Chatham-Kent / Southwestern Ontario — farmer-owned solar co-op)**:
   - **Ownership & governance**: Spun out by AGRIS Co-operative (a 100-year-old farmer-owned grain/input cooperative) and managed alongside Green Energy Co-operative of Ontario (GECO) by Red Jar Energy Partners; **1,000+ farmer and rural landowner members**.
   - **Scale & grid workaround**: Owns ~**15 MW of solar across 250 project sites**, including **20 multi-owner "Solar Gardens"** (ground-mounted arrays where multiple rural neighbours pooled capital on a single connection point to bypass rural distribution transformer limits under Ontario's FIT/microFIT programmes). No open hardware or open monitoring data published.

## Why it belongs

1. **It confirms the A1/A2/A5 governance pattern in farm energy**: where capital requirements ($12M RNG skids) or grid rules (transformer limits, FIT contracts) lock out single farms, the cooperative form (producer co-op in Warwick, community-bond co-op at ZooShare, farmer solar co-op at AGRIS, alongside Alberta's statutory Rural Electrification Associations like EQUS and the Federation of Alberta Gas Co-ops) is the working Canadian vehicle for collective ownership — and in every case it buys turnkey proprietary equipment rather than building or adopting an open technical commons.
2. **It highlights the institutional intermediary role (`Coop Carbone`)**: Warwick succeeded because a technical solidarity co-op (`Coop Carbone`) carried the engineering, grant-writing, and Énergir contract negotiation on behalf of 12 dairy farms — a structural lesson for both the dairy (`dairy/open-milking-robot-case-study.md`) and upcycling (`upcycling/concept.md`) workstreams.

## What it does not demonstrate

- Any open-source hardware, software, or open-data release by Canadian biogas or solar cooperatives.
- Replicability without state tariff/grant scaffolding: ZooShare and AGRIS Solar were built on Ontario's discontinued FIT/microFIT tariffs, and Warwick required $4.7M in federal/provincial public finance plus Quebec's mandated Énergir RNG purchase programme.
