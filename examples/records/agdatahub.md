# Agdatahub

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: France / European Union
- Project: https://agdatahub.eu/
- Field-guide context: new discovery from the data-cooperatives cycle, 2026-08-02

## Problem addressed

Agdatahub is an intermediation platform for agricultural and agri-food data: structuring data sharing (plots, weather, equipment, farming practices) between producers, manufacturers, and service providers under farmer consent. Its stated aim is to make data sovereignty a day-to-day reality for French and European agriculture, serving France's ~380,000 farms.

## Open layer

Consent-based data intermediation: farmers create a digital identity, manage and revoke authorisations independently, and grant access granularly; data flows through a neutral intermediary under the EU Data Governance Act (DGA) framework. The API-Agro open standard underpins the platform.

## What is actually open

- Consent routing architecture with granular farmer control; free access for farm users (business model: B2B fees for commercial data users).
- Listed on the European Union Register of Data Intermediation Services since June 2024 (DGA-aligned neutral third-party intermediary).
- Gaia-X Day-1 Member (French Hub) and participant in AgriDataSpace — connecting the platform to the EU agricultural data-space ecosystem.
- Product lines: Agdatahub Exchange and Agdatahub Consent & Connect.
- The platform software itself is not documented as open source; the openness is in the consent architecture and the API-Agro standard.

## Governance and control

"Agricultural and institutional governance": shareholders drawn from the agricultural sector — API-Agro SAS (a holding of 30 associates representing the sector), plus Avril, InVivo, Banque des Territoires (Caisse des Dépôts group), IN Groupe, and others. It is a company whose ownership is sector- and institution-based, not a farmer cooperative; the MDPI governance analysis (2026) calls it the most mature commercial model among the compared data-space initiatives, with farmers at the centre of the governance model's consent design.

## Evidence of use

- €4.8 million raise (Avril Group press) to develop the French and European "infratech" platform.
- Selected by Gaia-X to accelerate EU agriculture transformation; documented as a GAIA-X use case (German ministry page on "smart agriculture data exchange").
- MDPI 2026 governance analysis includes Agdatahub as a national-scale DGA data-intermediation implementation.
- Adoption scale across French farms was not verified in this pass.

## Maintenance and funding

Mixed model: sectoral investor funding + public support; free for farmers, B2B fees for commercial users. The company is headquartered with offices in Paris, Bordeaux, and Lyon.

## What this case demonstrates

Agdatahub is the strongest located example of DGA-aligned agricultural data intermediation: a sector-owned company implementing farmer-centric consent architecture at national scale, and the French/European counterpart to JoinData's cooperative model — same consent-first design, different ownership form (company with sector shareholders vs farmer cooperative).

## What it does not demonstrate

- It is not farmer-owned or farmer-governed; sector and institutional shareholders hold the company.
- Adoption scale and farmer-level outcomes are unverified.
- The platform software is not documented as open source.
- "Farmer at the centre" refers to consent design, not structural control.
