## Problem addressed

A university-grade succession-planning curriculum exists (business organization, succession planning for family farms) but is scattered across paywalled extension materials. This open textbook assembles it as a free, openly licensed educational resource.

## Open layer

**CC BY-NC-ND 4.0**, verified inside the chapter PDF (2026-11-18): "This work is licensed by Christy Anderson Brekke & Joe Hobson (©2019) under a Attribution-NonCommercial-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0)." Redistributable verbatim; **derivative works forbidden** — the one clause that blocks the jurisdictional and circumstance adaptation succession materials always need.

## What is actually open

- The full textbook (e.g. the "Getting Organized" chapter on business organization and succession planning for Oregon family farms and ranches, verified in-PDF) as downloadable chapters under an open licence.
- Chapter-level worksheets within the same NC-ND terms.

## Why it belongs

It documents the middle shape of the C1 licensing spectrum: genuinely open (not free-to-read-only, not member-gated), yet forkless by design. In the corpus's terms: the licence is open enough to redistribute but not open enough to adapt — which, for succession materials whose entire value is jurisdiction-specific (tax, rollover, entity law), makes the ND clause functionally equivalent to closure. Recorded as the contrast case that defines what the succession program's L1 layer needs: share-alike without no-derivatives.

## What it does not demonstrate

Canadian content or applicability; adaptation examples (none legally possible); adoption metrics.
