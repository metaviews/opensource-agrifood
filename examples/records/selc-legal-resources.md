# Sustainable Economies Law Center (SELC)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: United States (Oakland, CA) / solidarity-economy movements generally
- Project: https://www.theselc.org/
- Live docs: https://www.theselc.org/resources (organized by audience) and https://www.theselc.org/radical_real_estate_law_school_resources (Resources for Land Stewards)
- Field-guide context: the only organization found in the C3 pass publishing farm-adjacent legal resources under a genuine share-alike open licence
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

Legal knowledge for cooperative, commons, and community-owned enterprises is held by a small profession at professional prices. SELC's stated strategy is to publish "legal guides, resource libraries, sample documents, and even cartoon legal documents to make the law welcoming and navigable to everyone" (verified from our_strategies page).

## Open layer

Site-wide licence statement, repeated across its resource pages (verified 2026-11-18 on /resources and /cooperatives-resources): **"Feel free to share, use, and/or build upon any of these cartoons and these legal resources, which are licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0"** — share, adapt, and redistribute, with share-alike. The only genuine open licence on legal resources found anywhere in the farm-tenure landscape.

## What is actually open

- Legal guides, sample documents, bite-sized one-pager guides, toolkits, and policy reports across cooperative enterprises, housing, land, food, energy, mutual aid, and immigrant cooperatives.
- **Resources for Land Stewards** ("Radical Real Estate Law School"): "Legal tools to help you democratize land, housing, food, and energy" (verified page description).
- A Legal Cafe programme providing sliding-scale legal advice — the service layer alongside the published assets.

## Governance and control

Nonprofit law center; mission page carries governance and financial transparency. Not farmer-controlled; its constituency is solidarity-economy organizations broadly.

## Evidence of use

Not re-verified this pass (adoption counts not located). Its licensing practice, not its adoption, is what this record holds.

## Maintenance and funding

Nonprofit-funded (donations, programs); resources page actively maintained (a Legal Cafe event page dated 2026-11-19 was live at check).

## Why it belongs

It is the C3 pass's proof of concept that publishing legal documents under CC BY-SA is an existing, operating practice in exactly the adjacent sector — just not the farm-specific one. The succession program's L3 build (an open-licensed Canadian farm-tenure legal layer) would be doing for farm tenure what SELC already does for the solidarity economy, and its licence statement is the precedent text.

## What it does not demonstrate

No farm-tenure or succession-specific documents verified this pass; no evidence of legal-use outcomes; the licence statement is a site footer, not per-document deeds; US law jurisdiction.
