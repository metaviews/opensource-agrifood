# Harvest Food Hub (Harvest Algoma)

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: Northern Ontario (Sault Ste. Marie / Algoma District origin); expanding toward food banks across Canada (CTV, 2025-11)
- Project: digital food-rescue tracking platform — logs donations in, agency pickups, and distribution out of a regional food resource centre; built by the Sault Ste. Marie Innovation Centre (SSMIC) for Harvest Algoma, funded by an Ontario Trillium Foundation grant ($100,000 OTF)
- Field-guide context: opened via the A5 northern/remote food systems scan (G-OSA-39); verified 2026-09-09 — **licence unresolved, hence candidate not curated**
- Record type: software record — northern-built food-logistics infrastructure in the G-OSA-28 hub/logistics layer

## Problem addressed

Food banks and food resource centres run on spreadsheets and paper: donated food's journey (rescued from donors → sorted → agency pickup) is untracked, so waste can't be quantified, funders get anecdotes, and staff time goes to admin. The CTV/Northern Ontario Business coverage (Dec 2025) reports the app "slashing admin work" and tracking the donation journey end-to-end.

## What it does

Agency registration with partnership agreements; food logging with quantities and intended impacts; staff/volunteer activity management; reporting on food rescued and distributed across the district. Built by an Innovation Centre (a non-profit regional tech organization), not a commercial vendor — a public-interest build funded by an Ontario community grant.

## Open layer — UNVERIFIED, the reason for candidate status

No public repository, licence statement, or open-source claim was located in any coverage or on the platform portal (harvestfoodhub.ssmic.com) at verification time. The code appears to be held by SSMIC with no published terms. Two readings remain live:

- If SSMIC releases it under an open licence, this becomes the north's own contribution to the G-OSA-28 open-hub software layer (Foodsoft/OFN territory) and a northern-built open artifact — the most interesting outcome.
- If it stays closed, it repeats the Second Harvest pattern (A1-upcycling shape 1): publicly funded charity infrastructure, closed and potentially licensable.

The record is curated as a candidate specifically to hold this question open — the follow-up is a direct ask to SSMIC/Harvest Algoma about release plans, which the scan flags as fieldwork/correspondence rather than more web search.

## What it does not demonstrate

- Any open licence (none found — that is the open question, not a finding).
- Deployments outside Algoma beyond pilot conversations (national expansion is reported intent).
- Northern-remote specificity: Algoma is regional-north (road-connected); the app has no documented fly-in community deployment.
