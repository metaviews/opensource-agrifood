# Open Agroecology Lab (REGOSH)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: Argentina (Mendoza, Buenos Aires, Córdoba and other provinces); networked with GOSH and OpenTEAM internationally
- Project: https://regosh.libres.cc/en/projects/open-agroecology-lab/
- Field-guide context: new discovery from the Global South open-hardware cycle, 2026-08-02

## Problem addressed

Supporting the agroecological transition in Argentina through collective knowledge-building. Agroecology is described as a process-based, place-based production mode that cannot be served by the prescriptive input-advice model of conventional agriculture; it needs infrastructure for peasants, farmers, students, and researchers to build, systematise, and exchange knowledge.

## Open layer

Open and distributed infrastructure based on open-source technologies and participative practices, spanning farm data management and open sensing hardware.

## What is actually open

- Farm data layer: farmOS, SurveyStack (web-based farm management and soil-testing data entry), the farmOS-Aggregator (permissioned aggregation across farmOS instances), and Farm Coffee Shop (community benchmarking of farm indicators).
- Hardware layer (with partners): collaboration with Our-Sci and OpenTEAM to complete a high-throughput CO2 soil respiration device, and use of an open reflectometer; these are open science hardware collaborations rather than in-house designs.
- The lab's digital infrastructure was developed through the Code for Science and Society Digital Infrastructure Incubator, with co-designed data governance and a local ontology under development.

## Governance and control

Described as an "assemblage of rural organizations, researchers and activists": CEFIC (rural knowledge-exchange organisation), Ayllu cooperative, CRECE (Mendoza), and university faculties (UNCuyo Faculty of Agricultural Sciences, FCEN). Supported by the Shuttleworth Foundation and part of the GOSH community. Farmer/community co-design is a stated method; the data-governance ontology is still under co-design.

## Evidence of use

- Documented activities: first "Diálogo de saberes sobre suelos" workshop (2018); AgroPacha workshop series (2021, UROS projects); Residence of Open Technologies for Agroecology and Environmental Health in Mendoza (September 2022) with a proof of concept; Open-Climate fellowship (2023) on climate change and the lab's infrastructure.
- Collaborations with Our-Sci, OpenTEAM, and the Digital Infrastructure Incubator are documented on the project site.
- Activity since 2023 is unverified in this pass.

## Maintenance and funding

Foundation-funded (Shuttleworth) with institutional incubation support (CS&S). Ongoing funding and maintenance after the documented 2018-2023 activity window are unverified.

## What this case demonstrates

REGOSH is the strongest located Latin American open agroecology stack: a rural-organization-led assemblage connecting open farm-data tools (farmOS ecosystem) with open sensing hardware, and explicitly co-designing data governance with farmers. It extends the collection's farmOS thread into the Global South with a community-governed, agroecological framing.

## What it does not demonstrate

- It does not establish current activity (post-2023) or sustained funding.
- Farmer-control outcomes of the co-designed data governance are not yet documented (the ontology is under construction).
- Hardware output is collaborative (Our-Sci/OpenTEAM) rather than lab-owned.
- Deployment scale across Argentine farms is unverified.
