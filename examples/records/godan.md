# Global Open Data for Agriculture and Nutrition (GODAN)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: global; Africa-relevant agricultural and nutrition data coordination
- Project: https://www.godan.info/
- Field-guide context: `units/godan-2-0-africa-open-data.md`

## Problem addressed

GODAN addresses fragmented agricultural and nutrition data, weak coordination, and the need to make relevant data more accessible and usable.

## Open layer

Open-data coordination, frameworks, institutional networking, and public-interest infrastructure.

## What is actually open

GODAN is primarily a coordination and framework actor rather than a single software repository. Its current public framing connects open data with youth-led innovation, inclusive digital infrastructure, and food-system transformation.

## Governance and control

GODAN operates through international coordination and partner networks. The current public source does not by itself establish how farmers or communities participate in governance or benefit sharing.

## Evidence of use

The current site provides a live institutional framing and initiatives. Historical partnership, chapter, and impact claims require claim-specific sources.

## Maintenance and funding

Current institutional structure, funding, and measurable outputs require further verification. Public counters should not be treated as impact evidence where they are incomplete or placeholder values.

## Why it belongs

GODAN helps test what institutional coordination contributes beyond publishing individual datasets and how open-data principles travel into agrifood systems.

## What it does not demonstrate

GODAN’s coordination role does not by itself demonstrate that data are actually interoperable, reused, farmer-controlled, or beneficial at farm level.
