# Bhashini (National Language Translation Mission)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: India / national; 11+ regional languages in agrifood use (22 scheduled languages overall)
- Project: https://bhashini.gov.in/
- Field-guide context: none

## Problem addressed

India's farmers and citizens face language barriers in accessing government services and information. Bhashini is India's language DPI (MeitY) providing translation, ASR, TTS, and voice-to-text services so services — including agricultural schemes — reach users in their own languages.

## Open layer

Partial: state-run language AI platform with open-participation data collection (BhashaDaan), a models-and-datasets portal (Model & Data Vatika), and ULCA dataset infrastructure; API services to government and approved partners. The platform is state infrastructure with mixed openness — not a fully open dataset or model repository.

## What is actually open

Verified agrifood use (Kisan Divas page, 2026-08-03): the PM-KISAN chatbot (Kisan e-Mitra) is voice-based, supports 11 regional languages, and uses Bhashini voice-to-text for spoken farmer queries; coverage includes scheme information, crop insurance, financial assistance. Bhashini's own openness (ULCA terms, model licences, API access) was not audited in this pass (G-CV-02 in the Common Voice scan).

## Governance and control

State-run (MeitY, National Language Translation Mission); agrifood application is the PM-KISAN government service. Farmer data governance per state service terms; the DPI layer is state-controlled with commercial and startup participation (Udyami, Udbhav programmes).

## Evidence of use

PM-KISAN chatbot deployed with 11 regional languages and voice input; Bhashini celebrates Kisan Divas as an agricultural use case. Usage scale and farmer outcomes unverified beyond operational framing.

## Maintenance and funding

State-funded (MeitY mission mode). The institutional-hosting archetype applied to language infrastructure.

## Why it belongs

Bhashini is the language-layer DPI underwriting the largest located voice-based agricultural service (PM-KISAN chatbot) — the deployment shape of G-OSA-03's resolution, and a state-scale counterpart to the collection's other DPI records (AgriStack, WAGRI, CEADS).

## What it does not demonstrate

Bhashini does not demonstrate full openness (models/datasets partially closed), farmer-controlled governance, or verified farmer benefit; the PM-KISAN chatbot is a state service, not an open deployment.
