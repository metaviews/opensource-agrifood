# Herttoniemen ruokaosuuskunta (Herttoniemi food cooperative)

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: Helsinki, Finland
- Project: CSA-style ("kumppanuusmaatalous", partnership farming) food cooperative constituted as an osuuskunta since 2011, growing out of a Herttoniemi ruokapiiri (community food circle); maintains two "Omaa peltoa" (our field) plots producing harvest shares for members
- Field-guide context: opened via the grocery concept's Finnish co-op ecosystem survey (`grocery/research/finnish-co-op-survey.md`, 2026-11-25); single-source at this status
- Record type: governance record — the documented bridge from informal food circle to legal cooperative form in Finland

## Problem addressed

How an informal household food-buying circle (ruokapiiri) becomes a legal entity able to contract in its own name and run farming operations — the Finnish pathway from commons to cooperative.

## The open layer

**Governance arrangement, single-source.** The one substantive source found describes the model: "Osuuskunta lanseerasi kumppanuusmaatalous –mallin mukaisen toimintansa 2011 ja toimi inspiraationa lukuisille muille ruokaosuuskunnille Suomessa" — the cooperative launched its partnership-farming model in 2011 and served as inspiration for numerous other food cooperatives in Finland (Lab.fi teaching document, "Lähiruoan yhteistyömalleja maailmalta", Mikko Niipala, 8.6.2026). The same source records model extensions: self-cultivation options and a "farm-in-farm" concept letting members trial commercial market-garden operation.
