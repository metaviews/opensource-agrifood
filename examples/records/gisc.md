# Grower Information Services Cooperative (GISC / GiSC)

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: United States (Texas-origin, national); claimed worldwide producer reach
- Project: https://gisc.coop/ (maintenance mode as of 2026-08-03)
- Lineage: GiSC → Growers Ag Data Cooperative (GADC) / AgXchange (2017 merger with Ag Data Coalition) → NAPDC partnership (NIFA ODF grant 2023-2026)
- Field-guide context: none

## Problem addressed

Grower data ownership and aggregation: giving growers tools to index, store, protect, share, and use their own data, and to aggregate and benchmark across members for supply-chain leverage ("One person's data isn't worth a lot... when you put it together you can aggregate and benchmark" — Billy Tiller, 2020).

## Open layer

Cooperative data aggregation governance; cloud-based grower data repository (AgXchange platform via GADC). Not an open-source project: the open layer is the cooperative form and the claimed farmer ownership of aggregated data, not code or open data.

## What is actually open

- Grower-owned cooperative structure (per 2017 framing: "GiSC is the only grower-owned data cooperative in the United States"); a cloud-based data repository with standardized drop-down data entry and mobile access (2020 description).
- AgXchange: combined GiSC + ADC platform, "commercially available through the Growers Ag Data Cooperative (GADC)" (2017); Farmers Edge data-platform partnership (2017).
- Ag Data Transparent: the certification body Tiller helped found (9 seals incl. John Deere as of 2026) — the durable survivor of the GISC ecosystem.

## Governance and control

Grower-owned cooperative by design, governed by grower peers; merged into GADC (2017) and partnered with NAPDC (2023-2026 NIFA grant). Current governance and operational status: unverified — gisc.coop serves a maintenance-mode page (2026-08-03); no membership figures were ever published in any source located.

## Evidence of use

Founder Billy Tiller (Texas cotton farmer) testified to the US House Agriculture Committee (2015); presented at the SAOS conference (Jan 2020); Nebraska groundwater-compliance data work (smart meters, 2019-2020 era). No independently verified membership scale, platform usage, or farmer-control outcomes were located for any period.

## Maintenance and funding

Unverified. Cooperative/dues model per founding framing; current operational funding and activity unknown. Public founder activity concentrated 2015-2020; no recent public presence located.

## Why it belongs

GISC is the US reference case for the "grower-owned data cooperative" claim — the boundary between cooperative form and verified farmer control. Its trajectory (merger into GADC/NAPDC, maintenance-mode site, certification-body survivor) is evidence for the data-cooperative durability question (G-OSA-20 / Q-OSA-07).

## What it does not demonstrate

GISC does not demonstrate durable, independently verified farmer participation or control at scale: no membership or usage figures exist in the public record; the current operational status of AgXchange is unverified; and the maintenance-mode website is a negative freshness signal. The "only grower-owned data cooperative in the US" claim (2017) is unverifiable today.
