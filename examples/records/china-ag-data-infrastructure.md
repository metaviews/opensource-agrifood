# National Agricultural Scientific Data Infrastructure (China)

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: China (national)
- Project context: national scientific data center system; see https://english.cnic.cas.cn/platform/202303/t20230323_328657.html
- Field-guide context: new discovery from the China ag digital infrastructure cycle, 2026-08-02

## Problem addressed

China operates a national system of scientific data centers to share research data as public infrastructure. Agriculture is one of the domains within this system. The infrastructure addresses the fragmentation and enclosure of scientific data, including agricultural science data, and mandates open sharing as a national-interest policy.

## Open layer

National scientific data infrastructure: open data portals, technical standards, software stacks, and user portals for data and knowledge services across domains including agriculture.

## What is actually open

- Verified context: China's system comprises 20 national scientific data centers, developed on a national science and technology resource-sharing platform built by 42 research institutions across the Chinese Academy of Sciences, the Ministry of Education, the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs (MARA).
- The National Basic Science Data Center (CAS CNIC), the cross-domain center among the 20, reports 2.7PB of data resources, 34 management regulations, 57 specifications, and 23 national standards, and states it "develops technical standards, software stacks and user portals for open data and knowledge services".
- An agriculture-specific national scientific data center exists within the 20-center system; its portal and licence terms were not directly read in this pass (evidence limit).
- Policy context: China's scientific data-sharing policy has been documented as subordinating individual interest to national interest (National Academies presentation on China's scientific data policies).

## Governance and control

State-run (CAS, MARA, MOE, MIIT institutions). Data-sharing mandates are national-policy driven; governance is institutional, with no farmer-level or community control documented.

## Evidence of use

- The strategy literature documents the development and persistent problems of China's agricultural big data and information infrastructure (Sun et al. 2021, Chinese Academy of Engineering journal, cited 20 times).
- Agricultural science data sharing in China has been analysed since 2008 in the IFIP/CAAS literature.
- The basic-science center reports 4,000+ citations and support to 1,000+ key research projects; agricultural-domain equivalents were not verified in this pass.

## Maintenance and funding

State-funded. The 2020 archiving mandate for National Key R&D Program data (1,700+ projects archived by end-2022 at the basic-science center) indicates an active state data-curation programme.

## What this case demonstrates

China's national scientific data infrastructure is the state-run counterpart to the US Ag Data Commons and the EU data spaces in this collection: open data-sharing as national infrastructure, institutionally governed, with agriculture as one domain. It is the substrate on which open agricultural science data in China is supposed to circulate.

## What it does not demonstrate

- The agriculture-specific center's portal, dataset licences, and reuse terms were not read in this pass (candidate status).
- Whether agricultural science data sharing achieves practical openness (downloadable, reusable, licensed) is unverified.
- It is research-data infrastructure; it does not address farm operational data or farmer-level data governance.
