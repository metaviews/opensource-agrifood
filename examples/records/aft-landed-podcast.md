# LANDED — American Farmland Trust's farm-transition documentary podcast

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: United States (farm transition nationwide)
- Project: https://farmland.org/landed-podcast
- Live docs: premiere announcement + series page
- Field-guide context: the C11 pass's counter-model to lender content — succession knowledge as media produced by a sector nonprofit
- Last checked: 2026-11-19

## Problem addressed

Farm transition knowledge reaches families mostly through lender content operations
and paid advisors. LANDED makes land transfer itself the story.

## Open layer

Free-to-access and explicitly free-to-air (verified): "Radio stations are welcome to
contact AFT about airing this show at no cost." Distributed as a documentary series
mixing farmer, rancher, and advisor voices. **No licence grant found** — free-to-air
is distribution openness, not remix rights.

## What is actually open (verified 2026-11-19)

- The free-to-air offer, verbatim, on AFT's own announcement.
- The positioning: "LANDED explores how farms and ranches transition from one
  generation to the next."

## Governance and control

American Farmland Trust (nonprofit); editorial control internal.

## Evidence of use

No published audience figures exist (Rephonic third-party estimates only; ~7
episodes at time of the pass). Reach versus FCC's lender-content operation cannot
be compared from public data.

## Maintenance and funding

AFT organizational funding.

## Why it belongs

C11's cell 3 needed a non-lender model of succession knowledge published as media.
LANDED is the closest found example — and its gaps (no licence, no reach data) are
as instructive as its existence: the open-media model for succession knowledge is
demonstrated but unmeasured and unlicensed, which is exactly the space P6 proposes
to fill.

## What it does not demonstrate

Audience reach; an open licence (remix/redistribution rights); Canadian content.
