## Problem addressed

Farm succession planning lacks starter instruments a family can actually use — goal-setting, retirement budgeting, family-meeting structure, continuation checklists — as opposed to consultant deliverables or read-only guides. This workbook compiles exactly that: eleven worksheets plus a planning checklist.

## Why it belongs

C1's headline asset: in a landscape where every Canadian succession guide is free-to-read but closed (Crown copyright or silence) and every US university product is all-rights-reserved or CC BY-NC-ND, this is the one instrument found anywhere that a Canadian organization could legally fork tomorrow — adapt worksheets to Canadian tax/rollover content, add Canadian case material, republish under the same share-alike terms. It converts L1 from a "build-from-zero" problem into an "adapt-and-localize" one, and it is the existence proof that farm-succession funders will publish open.

## What it does not demonstrate

Canadian content (US tax, US legal frames throughout — adaptation is required, and is exactly what the licence permits); adoption metrics; digital-tool versions (worksheets are print-style).
