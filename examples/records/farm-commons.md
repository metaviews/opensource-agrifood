# Farm Commons

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: United States (farm-law education; nationwide US farm audience)
- Project: https://farmcommons.org/
- Live docs: https://farmcommons.org/library/ and licensing terms at https://farmcommons.org/about/licensing/
- Field-guide context: the sector's own farm-law legal-resilience organization — and the C3 pass's clearest case of openness used as a membership model
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

Farmers need sound legal grounding for business structures, sales contracts, leases, and employment — but farm-law expertise is scarce and expensive. Farm Commons provides "legal workshops, timely resources, and a community for farmers, ranchers, and their service providers" (verified from its home page).

## Open layer

Conditional and membership-gated. From the licensing page (verified 2026-11-18): "Farm Commons' resources and materials are protected by copyright, with the following qualification: **Institutional Members and Legal Professional Members of Farm Commons are granted a Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-ND 4.0) License** to utilize Farm Commons' resources." Plain-language summary: free to copy and redistribute, even commercially — but **no modifications may be distributed**, and the grant runs to members only.

## What is actually open

- A substantial farm-law library: business structure basics, farm sales agreements basics, detailed LLC operating agreement samples for farming couples (verified from library and the Farmers' Guide to Business Structures sample pages), podcast episodes, workshops.
- The licence that exists is NoDerivatives — the one term that blocks jurisdictional and circumstance adaptation, which is precisely what farm legal documents require.

## Governance and control

Nonprofit; membership tiers: Producer, Institutional, Legal Professional ($299/year, verified from membership page). Members fund and unlock; the organization controls.

## Evidence of use

Widely cited in US farm legal guidance (cited by ATTRA/NCAT CSA materials; its guides populate university extension lists — verified from third-party citations this pass). Adoption depth not re-verified.

## Maintenance and funding

Membership- and workshop-funded; active (fall 2025 workshop series live at check).

## Why it belongs

Two findings in one record. First, the sector's own legal-knowledge organization has already written most of the canon — LLC operating agreements for farming couples, sales agreements, leases — meaning the drafting work for an open farm-legal layer substantially exists. Second, its licensing choice (CC BY-ND, members only) is the most instructive negative case in the C3 pass: even the organization whose mission is farm legal resilience withholds the adapt-and-redistribute rights, so its library cannot seed the open adaptation layer the succession program's L3 envisions. Recorded as a contrast case, not an endorsement.

## What it does not demonstrate

Any open-licensed asset; no Canadian-law material; no evidence that the membership model funds new content creation (a plausible defence of the choice, recorded as unverified).
