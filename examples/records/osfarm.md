# OS Farm

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: France / EU; national interoperability consortium (France 2030)
- Project: https://www.osfarm.org/
- Field-guide context: none

## Problem addressed

French farm equipment is held by proprietary ecosystems with limited interoperation; farmers face costly, non-interoperable, non-repairable machinery. OS Farm proposes a "100% open source" agritech interoperability standard — normalizing software and hardware communication between any farm equipment (new and old) — so farmers can build their own technology hubs.

## Open layer

Open interoperability standard (API protocol, hardware component protocol, gateway, labelling protocol, central resource platform); built on AgOpenGPS + Centipède RTK + Ekylibre. Deployment is commercial: franchise/subscription model.

## What is actually open

Per the June 2023 France 2030 (AAP) synthesis deck: an open standard and resource platform (codes, notices, tutorials), with a consortium of 6 companies and 2 research labs. Claimed impacts: tractor fuel consumption -5 to -15%, equipment functional life extended from ~10 to 30 years, intrants -2 to -15%. Autoguidage kit at 4,990€ vs ~12,000€ market average. The deck is marked "Confidentiel"; post-2023 operational status (pilot farms, labelling-protocol use, deployment) is unverified.

## Governance and control

Company-consortium-led with research-lab support; decentralized franchise distribution with member commissions (5-20% of revenue). Not farmer-cooperative governance; farmers are the target users, positioned "at the centre" of the design narrative.

## Evidence of use

Site live (2026-08-03). The 2023 deck documents proposal-stage design (2 pilot sites, 50 beta-testers planned); no verified deployment results located.

## Maintenance and funding

France 2030 state investment programme (AAP), consortium-funded; subscription model per exploitation (3,500-9,500€/yr). French state as the anchor funder — the institutional-hosting archetype applied to an open interoperability standard.

## Why it belongs

OS Farm is the French state's answer to the OADA problem — an open interoperability standard for farm equipment — and the clearest located instance of a national government funding open agritech interoperability. It also extends the open-core + paid-services archetype into the French machinery layer.

## What it does not demonstrate

OS Farm does not demonstrate deployed open interoperability, farmer-controlled governance, or verified impact: the evidence is a proposal deck with planned pilots, current status unverified, and a commercial franchise model with "open source" attaching to the standard rather than the deployment.
