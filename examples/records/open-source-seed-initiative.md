# Open Source Seed Initiative (OSSI)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: global; seed breeders, farmers, and seed companies
- Project: https://osseeds.org/
- Field-guide context: `units/open-source-seed-initiative-africa.md`

## Problem addressed

OSSI responds to concentration in commercial breeding and restrictive intellectual-property arrangements that can prevent saving, replanting, sharing, or further breeding.

## Open layer

Biological materials, seed licensing, and commons governance.

## What is actually open

The OSSI Pledge preserves freedom to use pledged seeds and derivatives while requiring recipients not to restrict others’ use through patents or other means and to pass the pledge along with transfers.

## Governance and control

The initiative links plant breeders, farmers, seed companies, and sustainability advocates. Its pledge is a legal and institutional mechanism, not a software licence.

## Evidence of use

The current homepage reports more than 550 pledged varieties, 52 plant breeders, and 78 seed-company partners. These are date-stamped public claims; geographic distribution and farmer adoption require separate evidence.

## Maintenance and funding

OSSI identifies itself as a non-profit supported by donations and seed-company partners. The durability and geographic distribution of the model require further research.

## Why it belongs

OSSI makes it possible to examine openness beyond digital artefacts: biological reproduction, breeding freedom, seed sovereignty, and ecological stewardship.

## What it does not demonstrate

OSSI does not establish that a seed commons can be governed like a software commons, nor that its current public counts measure farmer adoption or African deployment.
