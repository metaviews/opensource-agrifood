# Statistics Canada — Agriculture–Population Linkage, table 32-10-0392-01

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: Canada (2021 Census of Agriculture linked to Census of Population, 25% sample)
- Project: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=3210039201
- Live docs: downloadable CSV via the Statistics Canada website (WDS)
- Field-guide context: the C9/C10 pass's headline open-data source — the only Canadian data found counting widowed farm operators by gender and age
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

The succession program's gender hypothesis — widows inheriting effective control as
the transfer wave proceeds — needed a datum. This linkage table counts Canada's farm
operators (and farm population) by marital status, gender, and age.

## Open layer

**Open data under the Statistics Canada Open Licence** (verified at source). The CSV
downloads freely; values were retrieved and recomputed by this program.

## What is actually open (verified from the downloaded CSV, 2026-11-18)

- Farm operators, widowed: **9,685 total — 4,005 Men+ and 5,680 Women+** (Women+
  share: 58.6%, derived and recomputed by this program).
- **9,245 of the 9,685 widowed operators (95.5%) are aged 55+** (Men+ 3,815; Women+
  5,430).
- Companion datum: total farm operators 262,045 in the linkage sample context;
  the full table cross-classes marital status × gender × age for farm operators
  and the farm population.

## Governance and control

Statistics Canada; national statistical agency; open licence.

## Evidence of use

Curated as the succession program's C10 headline datum: widows already outnumber
widowers among Canada's farm operators, and 95.5% of widowed operators sit in the
55+ band where transfers concentrate. The mechanism (widowhood → effective control →
succession decision power) remains an untested hypothesis — the table measures
marital status, not control transfer.

## Maintenance and funding

Statistics Canada census program; 2021 reference year.

## Why it belongs

It demonstrates the program's broader method on the demographic axis: the question
("could women's longevity shift power in agriculture?") was answerable, in part,
from open data nobody had assembled for this purpose — 58.6% of widowed operators
are Women+, a figure this program derived, recomputed, and published with its
arithmetic. It is also a demonstration of the open-data layer working exactly as
the land scan said the succession evidence base works: the evidence is open while
the mechanisms stay closed.

## What it does not demonstrate

Control transfer on widowhood (hypothesis, untested); non-operator women's
decision-making power (no census variable); causality; small-sample precision
(25% sample, rounded values).
