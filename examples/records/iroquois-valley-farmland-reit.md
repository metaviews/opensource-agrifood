# Iroquois Valley Farmland REIT

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: United States (national; organic and transitioning farms)
- Project: https://iroquoisvalley.com/
- Live docs: Public Benefit Report 2024: https://amberfarmland.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/IroquoisValley_PublicBenefitReport_2024.pdf
- Field-guide context: the C8 pass's patient-capital-at-scale model — community-funded farmland finance with a documented lease-to-purchase pathway
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

Beginning and organic farmers cannot buy farms at market prices from conventional
lenders. Iroquois Valley — a public-benefit B-Corp farmland REIT funded by individual
and institutional investors — buys farms and leases them long-term with a documented
purchase pathway.

## Open layer

Free-to-read Public Benefit Report documenting the products and scale; the offering
documents themselves are regulated securities (proprietary by nature). No open-licensed
financial model or term sheet released — the model is documented, the instruments are not.

## What is actually open (verified from the 2024 report, 2026-11-18)

- Scale: **131 investments, 36,000+ acres** as of December 31, 2024 (further counts
  verified as reported from the subagent pass).
- The product structure, verified verbatim: "We partner with farmers to offer
  **six-year leases, and in the seventh year, farmers have the option to either
  purchase the land or continue renewing long-term leases with us indefinitely**."
- Long-term mortgages (10-year, interest-only start) and operating credit lines as
  companion products.

## Governance and control

B-Corp REIT governed by its investor base (~950 individual/institutional investors per
the subagent pass); public-benefit structure but investor-controlled — recorded under
the definition document's value-capture test: capital is patient and mission-bound, but
control is not farmer or community governance.

## Evidence of use

Operating at scale since 2007 (per its own reporting); impact figures are self-reported
in its public benefit report.

## Maintenance and funding

Investor-funded; annual public benefit reporting.

## Why it belongs

It proves patient capital for farmland transfer can operate **at scale inside a
regulated securities structure** — 36,000+ acres with a lease-to-purchase pathway is
the largest non-family transfer-finance operation found in the pass. Its relevance to
the succession program is as the scale benchmark and the securities-structure caution:
the model's replication in Canada runs through securities law, and its instruments are
proprietary — documented but not forkable, the C8 pattern.

## What it does not demonstrate

An open instrument or model; succession-specific focus (it serves organic land access
generally — the succession framing is the program's classification); Canadian
replicability under Canadian securities law; verified social outcomes beyond
self-reporting.
