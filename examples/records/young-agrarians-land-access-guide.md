## Problem addressed

Land access knowledge for new farmers — the leasing and land-matching landscape —
published as a shareable guide.

## Open layer

Explicit, in-document open licence (verified from the PDF text layer): "This guide
is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 2.5
Canada license. We hope that you'll share it, reference it, remix it and share it
along the way (just as long as you do not sell it)."

## What is actually open (verified 2026-11-19)

- The licence line itself, verbatim, inside the document.
- The remix invitation in the same sentence — an organization speaking in
  open-culture idiom.

## Governance and control

Young Agrarians (nonprofit; the corpus holds its BCLMP and BC Transition Toolkit as
separate records).

## Evidence of use

Widely circulated land-access guide (5.0 edition); the YA land programs it supports
are separately recorded.

## Maintenance and funding

Nonprofit publication.

## Why it belongs

The C11 pass found that Young Agrarians' *succession* document (the BC Transition
Toolkit) carries no licence line, while this sibling guide carries a full CC
BY-NC-SA licence with an explicit remix invitation — inside the same organization.
The contrast is the pass's sharpest datum: it proves the licence gap around
succession knowledge is a choice or an oversight, not a capacity limit, and it makes
the fix askable ("one licence line on the toolkit").

## What it does not demonstrate

Licensing of the successor toolkit (recorded licence-silent); any succession
content of its own; NC/SA limits on commercial reuse.
