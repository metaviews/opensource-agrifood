- Status: `candidate`

## Problem addressed

AfricaOSH works to democratise science and technology in Africa through open science hardware: connecting makers, researchers, educators, students, hackers, and institutions, and showcasing open hardware developed by African and other global South innovators.

## Open layer

Community infrastructure: an annual summit hosted in a different African country, workshops and co-creation events, an EU-funded African-European maker innovation ecosystem programme, and membership of the Gathering for Open Science Hardware (GOSH) community.

## What is actually open

- The community's purpose is promoting open science hardware (designs publicly available to study, modify, distribute, make, and sell).
