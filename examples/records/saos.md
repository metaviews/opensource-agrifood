# SAOS (Scottish Agricultural Organisation Society)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: Scotland / UK; cooperative federation of ~56 primary agricultural co-ops (est. 1905; ~600 primary co-op directors; group turnover >£4.5M, 31 staff, McMahon 2022)
- Project: https://saos.coop/
- Data workstream: https://saos.coop/what-we-do/data/
- Field-guide context: none (cooperative data governance, not AI-focused)

## Problem addressed

Farm data is siloed, locked into vendor platforms, and its value accrues outside farming. SAOS works to deliver co-operative approaches to data management "that protect the rights of farmers" and unlock the value of precision farming for Scotland, including livestock traceability, herd-performance analytics, and a farmer-owned data-platform feasibility study.

## Open layer

Cooperative data governance and federation-owned data infrastructure. This is not an open-source project: the open layer is governance form (cooperative federation, subsidiary structure with deferred share ownership), not code or data.

## What is actually open

- ScotEID (est. 2008): Scotland's central livestock traceability database, operated by the wholly-owned SAOS subsidiary on behalf of the Scottish government and the livestock industry; 18 staff; expanded 2026 (bluetongue vaccination recording, Rural Payments and Inspection Directorate reporting, pig biosecurity pyramids). Statutory/regulatory data infrastructure, not open data.
- MyHerdStats: herd-performance insights derived from statutory cattle registration and movement data, provided to keepers.
- AMU (antimicrobial use) Pilot: vet-practice AMU reporting for farming clients.
- Data principles (George Noble, 2020): farmers own their data; farmer-first design; real-time insights; partnerships; trust.
- SmartRural subsidiary: feasibility study (2026) for a data-management platform "which could be used as a basis for farmer-owned data co-ops"; Scottish Agriculture Data Partnership announced late 2025 (social channels; governance details unpublished).

## Governance and control

SAOS is a cooperative federation owned by its member co-ops. Its data subsidiaries (ScotEID, SmartRural) are wholly-owned federation subsidiaries, not farmer-share-owned co-ops: share ownership is explicitly deferred ("as the subsidiaries mature... SAOS may well then move the 'ownership' to our more traditional understanding of co-op share ownership" — Yuill, McMahon 2022). A SAOS board member sits on each subsidiary board, with quarterly reporting to the SAOS Board. Farmer control is therefore mediated through federation governance rather than direct member ownership of data infrastructure.

## Evidence of use

ScotEID has operated since 2008 under government contract and was expanded in 2026 — the most durable located instance of farmer-organization-owned data infrastructure outside the Netherlands. Proven outcome: a multi-agency data collaboration (SAOS + NFU + government + abattoirs) that nearly eradicated BVD from Scottish cattle. The precision-ag farmer-owned data cooperative itself is feasibility-stage, not operating scale.

## Maintenance and funding

Federation-funded: group turnover >£4.5M (McMahon 2022), member services, and government contracts (ScotEID on behalf of Scottish government and livestock industry). One of the strongest maintenance pictures in the collection — the caveat being that the durable funding is for regulatory traceability infrastructure, not voluntary farmer data exchange.

## Why it belongs

SAOS is the strongest test of whether cooperative institutions can own agricultural data infrastructure durably — and the clearest case of ownership deferral between federation and members. It is the UK/Scotland answer to the data-cooperative question Q-OSA-07 asks.

## What it does not demonstrate

SAOS does not demonstrate a farmer-owned, member-share-controlled data cooperative at verified scale: the subsidiaries are federation-owned with share transfer deferred, the farmer-owned platform is feasibility-stage, and the Scottish Agriculture Data Partnership has no published governance. Direct member-level participation and control outcomes are unverified.
