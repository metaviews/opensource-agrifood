# Agri-food Data Canada

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: Canada; University of Guelph and research-centre ecosystem
- Project: https://agrifooddatacanada.ca/
- Semantic Engine: https://www.semanticengine.org/
- Field-guide context: Canadian discovery lead; not transferred from the Agrifood AI Field Guide

## Problem addressed

Agri-food Data Canada works to increase the value, discoverability, interoperability, and reuse of agricultural research data.

## Open layer

FAIR data practices, research-centre data portals, reusable data exploration, semantic tooling, machine-actionable schemas, and research-data training.

## What is actually open

The public site documents research-centre data portals, the RED-X Re-usable Data Explorer, and Semantic Engine tools for templates and machine-actionable schemas. The Semantic Engine uses the open, international, machine-accessible Overlays Capture Architecture (OCA) standard and supports schemas, agreements, machine-readable records, and data requests. Open licensing and access terms for each tool and dataset require separate verification.

## Governance and control

The initiative is hosted at the University of Guelph and works with participants in the research-data ecosystem. The public materials establish institutional stewardship and FAIR objectives, but not yet a farmer-governed data model or a fully open commons.

## Evidence of use

The project maintains a public site, tools, data portals, training activity, and current news. Some research-centre data portals require login, while other tools and visualisations are publicly available. The scope of users and participating research centres requires more detailed evidence.

## Maintenance and funding

Institutional hosting and active public releases provide a maintenance anchor. Funding, governance, and the relationship between university-controlled infrastructure and wider public reuse remain to be clarified.

## Why it matters

Agri-food Data Canada is a Canadian research-data infrastructure case distinct from Ag Data Commons. It foregrounds semantics, schemas, and research-centre data access rather than a federal repository alone.

## What it does not demonstrate

FAIR principles do not automatically mean open source, open data, unrestricted reuse, farmer control, or interoperability across all agricultural systems.
