# Sharemilking Agreements Order 2011 (New Zealand, SR 2011/295)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: New Zealand (national statutory instrument; the reference model for income-share farm entry worldwide)
- Project: https://www.legislation.govt.nz/regulation/public/2011/0295/latest/DLM3947702.html
- Live docs: full text on legislation.govt.nz (in force; latest version as at 2023-12-23; administered by MBIE)
- Field-guide context: the C8 pass's headline — a complete, statutory, ready-to-adapt income-share farm-entry contract published openly by a state
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

Income-share farm entry (sharemilking) needs a fair, complete contract: share floors,
feed and stock clauses, dispute resolution. Elsewhere these are drafted by lawyers or
locked in industry-association membership (DairyNZ: "Contracts are available from a
variety of sources: Federated Farmers, Lawyers, Farm Advisers"). New Zealand publishes
the standard agreement as secondary legislation.

## Open layer

**Full statutory standard agreement text, freely published** on legislation.govt.nz.
Crown copyright; no open-licence statement in the text — but as legislation, it is
public by nature, and republishing statutes with attribution is standard practice. The
closest thing to a public-domain-ready farm-entry contract found anywhere.

## What is actually open (verified from the full text, 2026-11-18)

- Complete standard agreement: minimum share floors ("The sharemilker must receive
  **not less than 21% of income** derived from milk supplied to the dairy company…
  The 21% is net of the sharemilker's farm operating expenses and **must not be
  reduced in any way or by any means other than under clauses 63 to 66 or by the
  result of dispute resolution as provided for under this Agreement**"), pasture-cover
  and closure schedules with fill-in fields, herd-owning variants.
- **Dispute resolution embedded in the instrument**: the share floor is protected
  against erosion except through the agreement's own dispute-resolution mechanism —
  the C7 finding's proof of concept at clause level.
- Herd-owning sharemilking (HOSM) structures codified, matching DairyNZ's documented
  equity-partnership ladder (equity partnership → herd ownership → farm purchase).

## Governance and control

New Zealand state (Parliamentary Counsel Office; MBIE administers). The contract is a
public instrument, not an industry asset — the structural difference from every
privately held farm-contract template found in the pass.

## Evidence of use

The operative statutory basis for NZ sharemilking agreements; the ladder it supports is
a documented NZ entry pathway, though 2025 research (verified as reported) finds the
traditional ladder weakening — "sharemilking opportunities have reduced and may not, on
their own, produce sufficient equity."

## Maintenance and funding

State instrument; versioned (4 versions; latest 2023).

## Why it belongs

It is the C8 pass's existence proof that a **state can publish a complete,
ready-to-sign farm-transfer-adjacent contract with fairness floors and dispute
resolution built in** — doing openly, for income-share entry, exactly what the
succession program's adaptation commons proposes for succession instruments. For
design-document P5, it is the import candidate and the template: a Canadian standard
agreement for non-family transfer, statutory or quasi-statutory, published with floors
and dispute resolution.

## What it does not demonstrate

An open licence on the text (Crown copyright, no re-use statement); dairy-specific
applicability; Canadian-law validity (adaptation required); that the model economics
still work (the NZ ladder is eroding at home).
