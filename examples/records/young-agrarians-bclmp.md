# Young Agrarians B.C. Land Matching Program

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: Canada (British Columbia; the corpus's Canadian farm-link anchor)
- Project: https://youngagrarians.org/tools/land/bc-land-matching-program/
- Live docs: program page + BCLMP evaluation (UBC Farm, 2022)
- Field-guide context: the C2 pass's best-in-class Canadian farm-link operator — free, publicly funded, and the only operator found reporting aggregate match outcomes
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

Matching retiring farmers' land with entrants is the farm-link function Canada
otherwise lacks; BCLMP has operated it since 2016 (piloting Quebec's methodology).

## Open layer

Free to participants ("There is no cost to participate" — verified) and publicly
funded (Sustainable Canadian Agricultural Partnership + regional funders). The
underlying inventory (U-MAP) is registration-gated; outcome reporting is public
aggregates. Not open-licensed data — but the best outcome transparency found among
any operator examined.

## What is actually open (verified 2026-11-18)

- **Public aggregate outcome reporting:** "We've made 437 matches on more than
  13,210 acres to date!" — plus per-match narrative stories. No other operator
  examined (FarmLINK.net, L'ARTERRE, Minnesota, PA, California FarmLink) reports
  matches this way.
- The BCLMP evaluation (UBC Farm, January 2022) publicly frames the program's
  success criterion as *preventing bad matches* ("mismatches in values, skills,
  business plan preparation, local markets") — a success definition the pass found
  nowhere else.

## Governance and control

Young Agrarians nonprofit (the corpus holds its BC Transition Toolkit as a separate
record); registration-gated participant data, organization-controlled.

## Evidence of use

437 matches / 13,210+ acres is the largest verified Canadian farm-link outcome
figure; the program operates across B.C. regions with regional funders.

## Maintenance and funding

Publicly funded through agricultural policy frameworks; the roadmap's event backlog
notes the program's dependence on nonprofit funding cycles.

## Why it belongs

It is the anatomy finding's anchor: even the best operator reports only self-defined
aggregate counts under no data licence, and its own evaluation locates failure in
readiness and trust, not in hidden criteria — the evidence base for C2's correction
of design-document P7 (outcome publication as accountability, not as a match-rate
lever). It is also the natural who-to-ask first contact for match definitions and
drop-off data.

## What it does not demonstrate

An open data layer; match definitions comparable across programs; drop-off and
conversion tracking (the evaluation's own gap); transferability outside B.C.'s
funding model.
