# Kallaama (Senegal agricultural speech dataset)

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: Senegal / West Africa; Wolof, Pulaar, Sereer (~10M native speakers)
- Project: https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01991 (RAIL@LREC-COLING 2024, CC BY 4.0)
- Field-guide context: none

## Problem addressed

Senegal's national languages are under-resourced for speech technologies — all the more so in agriculture. Kallaama produces and disseminates national-language corpora for speech technology development "in the field of agriculture."

## Open layer

Open speech dataset: 125 hours of transcribed, agriculture-focused recordings in each of Wolof, Pulaar, and Sereer; textual corpora for Wolof and Pulaar; a 49,132-entry Wolof pronunciation lexicon; designed for automatic speech recognition. CC BY 4.0.

## What is actually open

The dataset and resources as described in the RAIL 2024 paper (CC BY 4.0). The corpus itself was not downloaded or audited in this pass (G-CV-03); downstream ASR systems built on it are unverified.

## Governance and control

Research project (Gauthier, Ndiaye, Guissé); national-language corpus initiative. No farmer-community governance layer documented; the dataset is a public resource, not a governed commons.

## Evidence of use

Dataset released (2024) and published at RAIL@LREC-COLING 2024. No located deployed extension service built on it.

## Maintenance and funding

Research-funded; ongoing maintenance and expansion unverified.

## Why it belongs

Kallaama is the clearest located instance of an openly licensed agricultural speech dataset — the supply-side shape of G-OSA-03's resolution (open language data built explicitly for agriculture, in West African languages the collection otherwise lacks).

## What it does not demonstrate

Kallaama does not demonstrate deployment (no located ASR service built on it), farmer-governed development, or community benefit; it is a research dataset with an open licence.
