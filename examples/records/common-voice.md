# Mozilla Common Voice

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: global; multilingual and community-led
- Project: https://commonvoice.mozilla.org/
- Data collective: https://mozilladatacollective.com/
- Field-guide context: `units/mozilla-common-voice-african-languages.md`

## Problem addressed

Common Voice addresses the concentration of speech and language data in a small number of languages and institutions.

## Open layer

Community contribution infrastructure and publicly accessible speech and text datasets.

## What is actually open

The platform supports community contribution, validation, curation, and release of language resources. Exact terms and dataset composition should be checked for each release before making licensing or volume claims.

## Governance and control

The project is community-led in its data-creation model, with foundation-supported infrastructure. Community participation does not automatically resolve questions of consent, representation, downstream benefit, or control.

## Evidence of use

The current site describes the platform and reports 290 languages and growing. It does not establish a particular agrifood deployment.

## Maintenance and funding

The platform has foundation and partner support. Long-term language-community participation and the distribution of benefits require further study.

## Why it belongs

Common Voice is an upstream example of the language and voice infrastructure on which inclusive agricultural advisory or farmer-facing AI may depend.

## What it does not demonstrate

Common Voice is not itself an agricultural application. Its existence does not prove that a particular language dataset is suitable for agricultural extension or that communities benefit from downstream use.
