# LOOP Mission

- Status: `curated` (counter-example — Canadian upcycling flagship, fully closed)
- Region / reach: Montreal, Quebec; national retail distribution; US expansion
- Project: https://loopmission.com
- Last checked: 2026-09-07

## Problem addressed

The most visible Canadian upcycled-food company: founded 2016 by David Côté and Julie Poitras, LOOP rescues cosmetically rejected fruits and vegetables ("the outcasts of the food industry") and processes them into cold-pressed juices, sodas, iced teas, and wellness shots — 36M+ lb of produce upcycled to date (Food In Canada). An alumnus of Impact Canada's Food Waste Reduction Challenge. A second LOOP entity (Loop Resources) runs surplus diversion for Sobeys/Save-On-Foods alongside Mesh Exchange.

## Open layer

None. Formulations, sourcing contracts with wholesalers and processors, and the process core are proprietary. The process core is the instructive part: LOOP's scalability depends on **high-pressure processing (HPP)** — it owns five Hiperbaric 420 machines, described in interviews as the company's biggest capital outlay ("HPP is the biggest buy that we did as a company"). HPP equipment is proprietary Spanish-made (Hiperbaric) capital machinery; HPP access for others is sold as *tolling services* (democratized by fee, not by openness). The capital-intensive equipment layer, the supply relationships, and the brand are the moat.

## Why it belongs

The sub-cell scan's shape 3 needs its Canadian anchor: the valorization layer's Canadian champion demonstrates the full closed stack — closed sourcing, closed formulation, proprietary capital equipment, and (via the certification landscape) even the category definition paywalled. LOOP is what a successful upcycling operation looks like in the absence of any open layer, and it is the shape an open-counterpart (open formulations, shared HPP/co-packing access, cooperative ownership) would be measured against. Its dual identity — consumer brand (closed) plus diversion infrastructure (Loop Resources handling retailer surplus) — also shows upcycling and redistribution converging in one company.

## What it does not demonstrate

Failure or critique of the business — it is the category's success story. Upcycled lb figures are self-reported. The record is about the closed layer, not the operation's quality.
