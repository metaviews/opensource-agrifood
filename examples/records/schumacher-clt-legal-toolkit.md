# Schumacher Center CLT Toolkit of Legal Documents

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: United States (Great Barrington, MA); the community land trust movement's founding legal documents, published since the 1970s
- Project: https://centerforneweconomics.org/apply/community-land-trust-program/toolkit-legal-documents/
- Live docs: model lease full text at https://centerforneweconomics.org/apply/community-land-trust-program/cltsb-model-residential-lease/
- Field-guide context: the oldest complete published legal-document set for community land tenure; carries the resale-price formula the land scan found nowhere open-licensed
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

A community land trust needs bylaws, articles of incorporation, and a ground lease that separate land ownership (community-held) from improvement ownership (occupant-held) while capping resale prices. Getting these drafted from scratch is the largest single legal cost of forming a CLT.

## Open layer

Free to read and download (PDFs and full web text); no open licence stated on the toolkit pages.

## What is actually open

- **Complete founding documents** of the Berkshire Community Land Trust and the Community Land Trust in the Southern Berkshires: bylaws, articles of incorporation, model lease, background essays, and the full 1972 *Community Land Trust: A Guide to a New Model for Land Tenure in America* (Swann et al.) as PDF.
- **The model residential lease, published in full** (verified 2026-11-18) with `[CUSTOMIZE]` markers — drafted to be adapted. Its key mechanisms: lessee owns improvements, lessor holds land; **First Option Purchase Price = "local replacement cost less deterioration, obsolescence and damage… intended to remove the market price of the land from any calculation of sale price"** — the resale-cap formula, published in full; a Permitted Mortgage addendum (regulated lenders only, lessor permission required); **Section 9: Continuation of Lease on Death of Lessee** — directly succession-relevant; Section 10 covers divided households.
- Section II of the toolkit publishes the actual bylaws of two operating CLTs, not just models.

## Governance and control

Published by the Schumacher Center for a New Economics (501(c)(3), founded 1980), which stewards the CLT program and co-founded the first CSA farm in North America on a CLT (Indian Line Farm — the CLT + farmer-owned-buildings succession precedent). The documents are governance artifacts of operating trusts, not marketing materials.

## Evidence of use

The Berkshire CLT and CLT in the Southern Berkshires operate under these documents. The 1972 handbook seeded the US CLT movement. Broader replication evidence not re-verified this pass.

## Maintenance and funding

Donor-supported nonprofit; documents maintained as part of the program's publishing, no stated update schedule.

## Why it belongs

The C3 pass's sharpest single fact: the sector's oldest and most complete land-tenure legal documents — including the exact resale-price-formula clause the land scan recorded as closed everywhere — have been published free-to-read for decades, and still carry no licence permitting adaptation. The gap is not that the documents were never written; it is that no organization ever licensed them.

## What it does not demonstrate

No adaptation licence; US (Massachusetts) law assumptions throughout; the residential lease is housing-shaped (farm use is in the recitals and program texts, not this lease); no documented modern replication traceable to these files.
