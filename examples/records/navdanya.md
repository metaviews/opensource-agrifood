# Navdanya

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: India (network across 22 states) and Bhutan
- Project: https://navdanya.org/
- Field-guide context: new discovery from the biological-commons cycle, 2026-08-02

## Problem addressed

Navdanya (Hindi for "nine seeds") is a seed sovereignty and biodiversity-conservation movement founded by Vandana Shiva (from 1991), defending seed and food sovereignty for small farmers against corporate seed systems, biopiracy, and GMO/pesticide-dependent agriculture.

## Open layer

Movement-led seed commons and sovereignty infrastructure: community seed banks, a network of seed keepers and organic producers, the Bija Vidyapeeth (Earth University), and campaigns (Seed Satyagraha, Seed Freedom) resisting seed patents and restrictive plant-variety regimes.

## What is actually open

- Community seed banks conserving India's heritage seeds; the organisation describes conserving "climate-resilient seeds through more than 150 community seed banks" with a network across 22 Indian states and Bhutan.
- Advocacy and documentation: Seed Freedom Report, biopiracy-victories documentation, and educational programming.
- It is a sovereignty movement with managed seed banks, not an open-licence system; seed access is movement- and community-managed.

## Governance and control

Founder-led organisation (Vandana Shiva); network of seed keepers and producers. Governance and internal decision-making beyond the founder figure are not documented in the sources read.

## Evidence of use

- Inconsistent scale claims across sources: the site overview reports 111 community seed banks in 17 states; the homepage claims "more than 150 community seed banks"; Wikipedia reports 54 community seed banks. The three figures cannot all be current, and the divergence is itself a contested-claims finding.
- Independent coverage (One Earth, FoodPrint) documents the farm and the movement; academic treatment examines Shiva's seed-sovereignty advocacy.
- CSB-level evidence of conservation outcomes was not independently verified in this pass.

## Maintenance and funding

NGO/movement-funded via courses, donations, and organic commerce (Navdanya Organic). Long-term CSB maintenance is not independently documented.

## What this case demonstrates

Navdanya is the largest and most visible seed-sovereignty movement in the Global South, and the clearest case of movement-managed biological commons: seed banks as political infrastructure. Its inconsistent published counts make it a useful discipline case — scale claims must be checked against sources, not repeated.

## What it does not demonstrate

- The community seed bank count is inconsistent across sources (54 / 111 / 150+); none is independently audited.
- It is not an open-licence system; access is movement-managed.
- Founder-led governance and internal decision-making are undocumented.
- Conservation and farmer-outcome evidence is not independently verified (candidate status).
