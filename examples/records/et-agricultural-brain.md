# Alibaba ET Agricultural Brain

- Status: `candidate` (closed-platform contrast case)
- Region / reach: China (commercial, national reach)
- Project: https://www.alibabacloud.com/et
- Field-guide context: new discovery from the China ag digital infrastructure cycle, 2026-08-02

## Problem addressed

Alibaba Cloud's ET Agricultural Brain (launched at The Computing Conference 2018, Shanghai) applies company-developed AI — visual recognition, voice recognition, real-time environmental monitoring — to agriculture, initially pig farming and fruit/vegetable production. It is the dominant commercial form of agricultural AI infrastructure in China: a closed, company-owned platform layer.

## Open layer

None documented. The platform is proprietary; no source code, weights, datasets, or interfaces were located as open. The record exists as the contrast case for the cell: what the dominant non-open form looks like.

## What is actually open

- No open assets identified in this pass. The value of the record is the explicit absence: company-owned algorithms, company-controlled data flows, enterprise customers, and ecosystem lock-in (Alibaba's sales/logistics chain).

## Governance and control

Company-owned (Alibaba Cloud, Alibaba Group). Farm data flows to the platform; the press release describes the system connecting government, enterprises, and farmers through Alibaba's ecosystem. Farmers and cooperatives are users within a corporate stack.

## Evidence of use

- Primary press release (7 June 2018): adopted by Tequ Group (Sichuan pig farming, stated 10-million-pig target), Haisheng Group (55 orchards, ~4,000 hectares; ~10,000 acres of apple trees converged to the platform, claimed RMB20 million annual operating-cost savings), and Guoqiang Modern Farming (Shaanxi melon cooperative).
- Performance claims are estimates (sow production +3 newborns/year, unnatural death -3%, PSY to 32).
- Academic context: the SAGE study of industrial upgrading in China's hog sector documents ET Agricultural Brain's role in interactive automation.

## Maintenance and funding

Commercial cloud business model. The product's current status (active, evolved, or superseded within Alibaba Cloud's ET Brain suite) was not verified in this pass.

## What this case demonstrates

ET Agricultural Brain is the reference example of the closed platform layer that open agrifood infrastructure in China (and globally) competes against: open claims absent, farmer data flowing into a corporate ecosystem, and benefit captured at the platform level. It anchors the scan's "closed platform" current.

## What it does not demonstrate

- It does not establish current product status or deployment scale in 2026.
- The claimed productivity gains are company/enterprise estimates.
- It has no open layer; it cannot be evaluated on openness terms beyond their absence.
