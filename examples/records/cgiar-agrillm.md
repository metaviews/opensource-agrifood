# CGIAR / AgriLLM

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: Global South orientation; multilateral agricultural research system
- CGIAR: https://www.cgiar.org/

## Problem addressed

AgriLLM aims to provide agricultural AI tools grounded in scientific and local agricultural knowledge for smallholders, extension agents, researchers, and policymakers.

## Open layer

A publicly released agriculture-focused model artifact, with an announced intention to release additional data, evaluation, and document resources.

## What is actually open

AI71 has published `AI71ai/Llama-agrillm-3.3-70B` with downloadable model files, a model card, and usage instructions. The model card describes four intended public goods: fine-tuned LLMs, supervised agricultural Q&A data, an evaluation benchmark, and an agricultural document corpus. The release status and licence terms of all four components require separate checking.

## Governance and control

The initiative is a multilateral and partner-led collaboration involving CGIAR, AI71, and other organisations. Its smallholder and Global South orientation is significant, but public materials do not yet establish farmer or community governance over the model, data, or deployment.

## Evidence of use

CGIAR documents 2025 workshops that generated agricultural Q&A pairs and describes a planned AI assistant. The public model artifact demonstrates release, not widespread deployment, agricultural impact, or local-language performance in practice.

## Maintenance and funding

The initiative depends on institutional and partner support. Long-term maintenance, release governance, compute access, and value capture remain open questions.

## Why it belongs

AgriLLM is an important boundary case for the collection: it connects open models, agricultural knowledge, multilateral infrastructure, local relevance, and public-interest AI. It also tests whether “open agricultural AI” includes the data, evaluation, and governance layers required for accountability.

## What it does not demonstrate

A downloadable model does not by itself establish open training data, open code, unrestricted reuse, accountable deployment, or farmer benefit. The underlying Llama terms and any model-specific restrictions matter.
