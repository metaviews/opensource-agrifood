# Fordhall Community Land Initiative (Fordhall Organic Farm, UK)

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: United Kingdom (Shropshire, England)
- Project: https://www.fordhallfarm.com/buy-a-share
- Live docs: 2026 share offer page; community-benefit-society structure
- Field-guide context: the C8 pass's community-shares-fund-the-succession-sale precedent — citizens bought the farm from development, the farmer holds a 100-year tenancy
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

A tenant farmer facing the sale of the farm he farmed needed a buyer other than
developers. Fordhall's answer: a community benefit society raised share capital from
thousands of citizens to buy the freehold, then leased the farm back to the farmer on
a century-long tenancy — community capital converting a threatened sale into permanent
agricultural tenure.

## Open layer

Free-to-read share offer and structure documentation; the share offer document itself
is proprietary. No open-licensed model or template — like every community-capital
instrument in the pass, documented but not forkable.

## What is actually open (verified 2026-11-18)

- The structure: "The Fordhall Community Land Initiative has existed since 2005…
  currently owns 128 acres of land and buildings at Fordhall Organic Farm, renting the
  land to a tenant farmer on a **100-year Farm Business Tenancy**" (verified from the
  share page).
- Operating duration: "we celebrate our **20th anniversary** of saving Fordhall Farm
  from development" (verified).
- Shareholder scale (~8,000, per the C8 subagent pass — verified as reported).

## Governance and control

Community benefit society; one-member-one-vote citizen shareholders; the farmer holds
security through the FBT, not through ownership. The separation — community owns,
farmer farms, contract secures — is the structure's exportable core.

## Evidence of use

Twenty years operating; the model predates and parallels the UK CLT network's rise
(the corpus holds the National CLT Network's model rules as a separate record) and the
corpus's community-bond precedents (ZooShare).

## Maintenance and funding

Community share capital; trading revenue from the farm's organic and education
operations.

## Why it belongs

It is the succession-specific community-capital precedent: the sale that had to happen
was funded by citizens, and the farmer's security is contractual (100-year FBT) rather
than proprietary. For Canada, it is the reference structure for a community-shares
farm-savings vehicle — with the C8 pass's flagged open question being Canadian
securities-law treatment of withdrawable community shares.

## What it does not demonstrate

An open-licensed instrument; verified shareholder count at source; transferability to
Canadian securities law; replication beyond this flagship (the model's diffusion
evidence is thin in this pass).
