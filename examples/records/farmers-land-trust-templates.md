# The Farmers Land Trust — Farmland Commons templates

- Status: `curated`
- Region / reach: United States (national nonprofit)
- Project: https://www.thefarmerslandtrust.org/templates/
- Live docs: templates page + Farmland Commons Process page https://www.thefarmerslandtrust.org/process/
- Field-guide context: the sector's only self-described "open-source" farmland-tenure template set — whose openness arrives wrapped in an adoption covenant
- Last checked: 2026-11-18

## Problem addressed

Farmland transfer into community-held tenure needs complete legal document sets (articles, bylaws, leases, donation instruments). The Farmers Land Trust publishes template documents "created from decades of experience in farmland transfer, protection, and tenure work rooted in community," built with Sustainable Law Group's Land Clinic and an extensive reviewer network.

## Open layer

Self-described open source, with a custom **use agreement** rather than a standard licence. From the templates page (verified 2026-11-18), downloaders: "(1) acknowledge [they] have read and are in general agreement with the 'Why a Farmland Commons' framework, (2) agree to utilize the name Farmland Commons and credit 'The Farmers Land Trust' in connection with **all uses** of these documents and the information contained therein, and (3) acknowledge and accept the Farmland Commons being provided on an open-source basis under a **non-commercial Creative Commons license**." A separate definitions page supports the templates.

## What is actually open

- Template legal documents (the specific documents are behind the use agreement; the page lists "Framing Guidance" sections — full inventory not enumerated on the public page excerpt this pass).
- The Farmland Commons Process documentation as the required companion.

## Governance and control

Nonprofit land trust. The use agreement functions as an adoption covenant: users take the name and the framework along with the documents. This is openness as brand-and-framework expansion — closer to a franchise licence than a commons licence. Under the definition document's control/funding/value-capture test, value capture flows back to the parent through mandatory naming.

## Maintenance and funding

Donation-supported nonprofit (Walden Mutual Bank partnership on the giving page); no update schedule stated.

## Why it belongs

It documents a third openness shape for the program's honesty section, beyond Farm Commons's membership model: openness as **adoption covenant** — real documents, real "open-source" self-description, non-commercial CC terms, but conditioned on framework agreement and mandatory branding in all uses. For the succession program's L3, it is the closest self-declared open farm-tenure template set, and its conditions are exactly what a genuinely open layer would have to negotiate or strip.

## What it does not demonstrate

A standard, deed-verifiable Creative Commons licence (the "non-commercial Creative Commons license" is not identified by version or deed on the page checked); adoption evidence; Canadian-law applicability.
