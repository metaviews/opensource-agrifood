# Second Harvest Food Rescue App (licensing model)

- Status: `curated` (counter-example — closed and monetized rescue infrastructure)
- Region / reach: Toronto, Ontario, Canada; national operations; licensing offered internationally
- Project: https://www.secondharvest.ca/licensing / https://foodrescue.secondharvest.ca/
- Last checked: 2026-09-07

## Problem addressed

Canada's largest food rescue organization (charitable registration #13386 5477 RR0001) runs the country's flagship surplus-redistribution logistics platform: since launch in 2018 the Food Rescue App has, by its own reporting, helped provide 226 million meals — matching food businesses with donees, dispatching rescue logistics, and producing the audit trail donors and regulators require.

## Open layer

None. The app is proprietary, and the decisive detail is what Second Harvest does with it: **it licenses the platform out as a commercial product** — "License the Second Harvest Food Rescue App to power scalable, branded food rescue solutions customizable for your community, business, or nonprofit impact" (secondharvest.ca/licensing, read 2026-09-07), with a booking/demo funnel and Salesforce-based field tooling (Flowfinity) behind it.

This is rescue infrastructure operating on the SaaS playbook: a charity-built platform whose scarcity model is exclusivity. The licence page's framing — "With greater adoption, this technology can transform how communities around the world tackle food insecurity" — states a universalist goal delivered through a proprietary gate.

## Why it belongs

The sub-cell scan's shape 1 (open claims without open artefacts) and its Canadian frame both need this record: the country's dominant rescue infrastructure is not merely closed (like FoodHero, Copia) but actively monetized as licensed technology by a registered charity. When the corpus asks who controls the diversion layer (grocery food-waste files), the answer in Canada is: one organization, gatekept by commercial licence. An open-rescue-stack argument must be made against this incumbent.

## What it does not demonstrate

Anything about the quality or reach of the rescue work (the meal figures are self-reported but plausible and widely cited). The record is about the licensing model, not the operation.
