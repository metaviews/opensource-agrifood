# Salling Group developer portal

- Status: `candidate`
- Region / reach: Denmark (Salling Group: Netto, Føtex, Bilka — Denmark's largest grocery retailer)
- Project: developer.sallinggroup.dev — the Nordic grocery sector's only official public developer portal, with REST APIs covering stores, products, and markdown/food-waste offers
- Field-guide context: opened via the Finnish co-op survey's follow-up pass (`grocery/research/finnish-co-op-survey.md`, addendum 2026-11-25) as the counter-model to the co-ops' data absence
- Record type: contrast record — corporate data openness where cooperative ownership produced none

## Problem addressed

The corpus's Nordic finding is that every consumer-owned grocery federation — S-ryhmä (Finland, 49% share), Coop Sverige, Coop Danmark, Coop Norge — publishes no open data, no developer portal, and no public API, despite owning the transaction data of millions of member-owners. The one Nordic grocery chain that opens a data surface is the private, shareholder-owned market leader.

## The open layer — and the honest finding

- **A public API surface exists**: "Enrich your app with Salling Group's public APIs" (portal landing page, verified live 2026-11-25).
- **Scope**: stores, products, and markdown/food-waste offers — the food-waste/discount surface connects directly to the corpus's food-waste legibility finding (waste streams measurable but opaque; here a retailer exposes its markdown inventory programmatically).
- **The honest limits**: "public" here is access-level openness. The API terms sit behind developer sign-in and were not inspected; no open licence over the data was found; nothing indicates the underlying datasets are redistributable. This is an interface, not an open-data release — the same interface-open/licence-closed pattern the corpus's G-OSA-22 vendor pass documented in CEA.

## Why it belongs (as candidate)

1. It is the sharpest available contrast for the co-op data-absence records (s-group-finland.md and the survey's Nordic addendum): shareholder-owned retail opened a programmatic surface; customer-owned retail at even larger scale opened none.
2. The markdown/food-waste API is a working precedent for what an open grocery operation's waste-legibility layer could expose — from the least likely governance form.
3. It corrects the corpus's frame: openness in grocery retail data is not co-op-averse, it is incumbent-optional — governance form predicts nothing.

## What it does not demonstrate

- Open-licensed or redistributable data — terms uninspected; no licence statement found.
- Co-operative or member governance of any kind.
- That the APIs are durable, documented to a standard, or free of sign-up gates.
