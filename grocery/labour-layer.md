# Open labour: a draft sixth layer

Status: draft (2026-09-07). This document extends, and does not replace,
`levels.md` — the authoritative text of the five layers. It exists because
the parent corpus's labour scan (`research/2026-09-labour-layer-scan.md`,
G-OSA-36) found the layer the five do not name, and because the
co-existence principle below supplies the argument for adding it.

## The principle

The eater and the worker need to co-exist. Both must have power, agency,
and participation. They may or may not be interchangeable depending on the
context — and the operation must be honest about which case it is in.

The five layers take the eater/participant as the person openness
empowers. The labour scan showed what that omission costs: an operation
can be open at all five layers while its workers have no more control over
their conditions, their data, or their time than before. Openness for the
people the operation serves is not openness for everyone the operation
touches.

## The layer, stated

**Open labour**: who does the work, on what terms, and can the workers see
and shape those terms. The operation's relationship to the people who do
its work — conditions, schedules, pay structure, safety, and the data
their work generates — is inspectable and shapeable by the workers
themselves. Labour is not the black box that open structure refuses to be
for everything else.

As with each of the five, this is a direction and a diagnostic, not a
compliance bar: the layer asks what is open, at which layer, and what is
refused.

## Interchangeability: a variable, not an assumption

The two subjects circulate in some operations and never meet in others.
Both are legitimate; they have different consequences.

**Circulating / interchangeable.** CSN and La Osa members working shifts;
Digger's Mirth workers eating what they grow; CoopCycle couriers
governing the platform they ride for; Landare's member-run floor. Here
eater-power and worker-power are the same power held by the same people,
and the five layers nearly suffice — participation can reach the workplace
because the participants are in it.

**Structurally distinct, never meeting.** A SAWP worker in Leamington and
a Flashfood shopper in Toronto; a greenhouse worker and a CSA member 500
kilometres away. Here the eater's five layers say nothing about the
worker's conditions — and the worker almost always has less exit power
than the eater (tied visas, thin rural labour markets, tied housing). An
operation can be fully open to its eaters while completely closed to its
workers. This is the normal case in the existing food system, and it is
the case the six-layer lens exists to catch.

The honest operation names which case it is in. The dishonest one claims
the first while practising the second.

## How the layer relates to the other five

Reinforcement: worker-governed operations make the other layers real
rather than decorative — couriers who govern the platform enforce honest
delivery data; member-workers who stock the shelves know the sourcing.
Destruction: the decoupling modes are the scan's four shapes — an open
operation running closed, surveilled work behind the counter (the
employer-side labour-software market: PickApp, FieldClock); worker
ownership without tooling (Digger's Mirth); the worker-held-data layer
that exists nowhere (WeClock is the only instrument, general-purpose, and
has never reached a farm).

The software mirror: the grocery method predicted that empty layers in
the free-software stack locate the frontier. The labour layer is empty —
no free-software implementation of on-farm labour governance exists
anywhere — which places the frontier exactly here.

## What this layer is not

- Not a claim that every operation must employ its eaters or be staffed
  by its members. The non-interchangeable case is real; the layer's demand
  there is openness toward the worker, not merger with the eater.
- Not the dissolution of the consumer co-op into a worker co-op. The
  demand is co-existence with power on both sides.
- Not a slogan. "We're all workers here" is the failure mode when the
  claim substitutes for the structure. The layer is measured by what
  workers can see and shape, not by what the operation says about dignity.
- Not a substitute for labour law or unionization. Openness instruments
  operate alongside legal protection (and are weakest where legal
  protection is weakest — which is why the migrant-labour case is the
  layer's hardest test).

## What remains unresolved (deliberately open)

- Whether the layer's diagnostic force survives translation from grocery
  to the rest of the chain (processing, land, fisheries — the parent's
  other cells each have a workforce).
- Whether interchangeability is binary or a spectrum, and what the middle
  cases look like (partial member-labour, seasonal circulation, gig
  workers who are also customers).
- What the worker-side data instrument for agrifood actually is — WeClock
  is the proof of concept, not the answer; the empty layer has no
  farm-shaped implementation.
- Whether the parent corpus adopts the six-layer lens or the lens stays
  a grocery-concept instrument offered to the parent (the sharpen-vs-build
  decision, still open).
