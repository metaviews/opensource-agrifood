# Grocery concept — Canadian co-op inventory research

Date: 2026-08-26
Scope: Canadian co-op directory infrastructure and named food co-ops.

This is a beginning of a Canadian co-op inventory, not a full survey. Sources are
extracted pages and follow-up search results. Claims are grounded where the source
supports them and flagged as thin where they are not. Cross-cutting findings live in
`research-mapping.md`.

---

## Canadian co-op directory infrastructure

From canada.coop/en/directory-and-map-enhancements/ (Co-operatives and Mutuals Canada,
CMC, Dec 17, 2021):

- "There are approximately 7,500 co-operatives presumed to be in business in Canada, 91% of
  which are non-financial, 8% are financial and 1% are mutuals."
- "CMC is proud to represent 40% of all co-operatives in Canada."
- "Our database powers the searchable, interactive Co-operation across Canada directory and map
  featuring the organization's name, address, website, co-op sector and type, and date of
  incorporation (where data is available)." (crm.canada.coop/en/map)
- "New DEI and procurement search functions are now available. The DEI tagging allows users to
  search for co-ops, credit unions, caisses populaires and mutuals that are comprised of members
  from, or primarily serve under-represented communities (i.e. Indigenous, ethnocultural groups,
  women, youth, LGBTQ2S.)" "The procurement coding allows users to search for various products and
  services offered by Canadian co-ops (i.e. retail, food, accommodation, etc.)"
- "CMC maintains the only comprehensive database of co-operatives, credit unions, caisses
  populaires and mutuals in Canada."

From ontario.coop/co-op-directories (Ontario Co-operative Association): co-op directories for
Ontario.

**Finding for the concept.** There is a real Canadian co-op directory infrastructure (CMC's
"Co-operation across Canada" map, with ~7,500 co-ops, DEI tagging including Indigenous, and
procurement coding including food). This is the existing infrastructure for a Canadian co-op
inventory — the concept has a concrete place to go for a Canadian grocery-co-op survey. The DEI
tagging (Indigenous, ethnocultural, women, youth, LGBTQ2S) is directly relevant to the concept's
culture/meaning and food-justice layers.

## Named Canadian food co-ops

From Wikipedia "List of food cooperatives," Canada section:

- Karma Co-op (Toronto, Ontario): "One of Canada's oldest food co-ops, it focuses on local,
  sustainable, and organic foods."
- Kootenay Co-op (Nelson, British Columbia): "A member-owned co-op known for natural and organic
  foods."
- East End Food Co-op (Vancouver, British Columbia): "A community-focused store offering local and
  fairly traded products."
- Ottawa Valley Food Co-op (Ottawa, Ontario region): "An online co-op that supports local farmers
  and producers."
- Co-op Atlantic (Eastern Canada): "While some of these stores have changed over time, they
  originally had a strong co-op tradition, linking consumers and producers."

Also visible across the Wikipedia list (for broader context, not Canada-centered corpus):

- France: La Louve (Paris), Superquinquin (Lille), La Cagette (Montpellier)
- Germany: FoodHub (Munich), SuperCoop (Berlin), köllektiv eG (Cologne)
- Ireland: Dublin Food Co-op (Dublin City), The Urban Co-op (Limerick)
- UK: Daily Bread Co-operative (Northampton, Cambridge), Essential Trading Co-op (Bristol),
  Infinity Foods Workers Co-operative (Brighton), The People's Supermarket (London), Unicorn
  Grocery (Manchester)

**Finding for the concept.** Canadian food co-ops are real and named (Karma Co-op as one of
Canada's oldest, Kootenay Co-op, East End Food Co-op, Ottawa Valley Food Co-op, Co-op Atlantic).
The corpus now has a concrete set of Canadian food co-ops to engage, plus the CMC directory
infrastructure for a fuller survey. The European names are useful context but not primary for the
Canada-centered corpus.

## Items thin or unverified in this pass

- The Wikipedia list is "not exhaustive" and "limited to notable food cooperatives." A proper
  Canadian survey would use the CMC "Co-operation across Canada" directory (crm.canada.coop/en/map)
  and the Ontario Co-op Association directories (ontario.coop/co-op-directories).
- The named co-ops have not been individually researched for their participation models, sourcing
  practices, or cultural/meaning dimensions.
- The CMC directory has not been queried directly for food-sector co-ops; the DEI and procurement
  search functions are noted as available but not exercised in this pass.
- Karma Co-op is noted as "one of Canada's oldest" and "about 800" members (from a Toronto Star
  piece referenced in search), but the concept has not done a deep dive on Karma specifically.
