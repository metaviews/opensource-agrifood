# Sources and provenance

This directory records the source practice for Open Source Agrifood. It is deliberately separate from the Agrifood AI Field Guide, which remains an adjacent research base and discovery aid:

https://agrifood.metaviews.ca/

## Source hierarchy

1. Primary project, institutional, standards, repository, legal, and policy sources.
2. Peer-reviewed research and independent case studies.
3. Credible secondary reporting used to establish context or deployment evidence.
4. The Agrifood AI Field Guide as a comparative synthesis and lead register, never as the sole evidentiary endpoint.

## Claim discipline

Every substantive claim should be traceable to a URL or a clearly identified local source. A source may support only a narrower claim than the surrounding synthesis. Do not turn a project description into evidence of adoption, impact, geographic scale, or sustainability without a source that establishes that specific point.

Use these verification labels:

- `verified-primary`: claim checked against the project or institution's own current source;
- `verified-independent`: claim checked against an independent or academic source;
- `field-guide-lead`: field-guide material identifies a lead, but original verification is incomplete;
- `stale-or-uncertain`: source exists, but status, date, scale, or interpretation needs another check;
- `not-evidence-of-scale`: useful description that does not establish deployment or adoption scale.

## Freshness model

- Facts, statistics, claims, and examples: re-check annually.
- Frameworks and conceptual material: re-check every two years, or sooner if the institution changes its position.
- Current project status, licences, repositories, and deployment claims: re-check annually and after a major release or organisational change.

## Attribution

Where Open Source Agrifood adapts CC BY 4.0 material from the field guide, preserve attribution and identify the adapted material. The field guide's code licence does not automatically apply to prose, datasets, images, models, seed materials, or third-party assets.
