## Why a v3

Since v2 (which rested on 58 records), the discovery programme expanded into eight major physical, biological, diagnostic, and logistical production cells. The corpus expanded from software, machinery, and language into **the full agronomic arc**: protected cultivation, water hydraulics, livestock pasture, post-harvest refrigeration, seed cleaning, short-chain distribution, agroforestry remote sensing, and field spectroscopy diagnostics. 

This expansion directly resolved five additional structural questions:
1. **The Protected Cultivation & Post-Harvest Boundary**: Closed climate computers and commercial walk-in refrigeration are challenged not by enterprise open copies, but by smallholder open hacks (`Mycodo`, `ColdSnap` $25 A/C override, `Real Seeds` air-column seed cleaner).
2. **The Livestock Enclosure Monopoly**: Active virtual shock fencing (`OpenFence`) is confirmed functionally absent and dormant in the open layer, leaving animal control heavily enclosed behind proprietary venture subscription collars (Nofence, Vence/Merck).
3. **The Multi-Strata Perennial Reality**: Agroforestry breaks the 2D field-polygon paradigm through UN FAO planetary remote sensing (`SEPAL` / `Open Foris`, MIT) and open QGIS plugins.
4. **Platform Cooperativism & Legal Innovation**: Short-chain food logistics pioneered **"Coopyleft"** (`CoopCycle`), legally restricting software use to democratic worker cooperatives to prevent gig-economy enclosure.
5. **Participatory Calibration Commons**: Open-science hardware (`Our Sci Reflectometer`, `OpenFlexure Microscope`) proved that sub-$400 open instruments achieve peer-reviewed field accuracy ($\pm 0.3\%$ SOC) by pairing open hardware with crowd-sourced open calibration datasets.

## Headline Finding

**The open agrifood layer has achieved robust, peer-reviewed, and production-proven viability across decentralized small-scale interventions, scientific simulation engines, and cooperative logistics, but hits a hard capital and biological enclosure wall at broad-acre industrial control.** 

Across 79 records, the landscape resolves into five structural realities:
1. **High-Durability Open Anchors**: Where physical requirements allow modular self-build or scientific transparency, open tools achieve decades of active production use — `OpenSprinkler` (CERN-OHL-S-2.0 / GPL-3.0 irrigation), `AquaCrop-OSPy` (Apache-2.0 crop-water modeling), `Open Foris / SEPAL` (UN FAO canopy MRV in 180+ countries), and `Open Food Network` (AGPL-3.0 in 20+ countries).
2. **The Capital & Liability Enclosure Wall**: Where technologies require massive capital tooling, high-voltage broad-acre SCADA, or high biological/animal-welfare liabilities, open source remains absent or dormant — commercial greenhouse climate computers (Priva/Hoogendoorn are interface-open, license-closed per C-OSA-06), active virtual livestock fencing (Nofence/Merck monopoly), and broad-acre center-pivot telemetry.
3. **The "Open Hack" De-escalation Strategy**: Smallholders bypass multi-thousand-dollar equipment costs through high-leverage open-hardware retrofits — `ColdSnap` ($25 open microcontroller overriding retail A/Cs for 34°F storage) and `Real Seeds Cleaner` (domestic vacuum + acrylic tube enabling community seed processing).
4. **Legal Innovations for Governance Protection**: Practitioners are actively moving beyond standard permissive licenses to defend governance against corporate capture — `CoopCycle`'s **Coopyleft** (restricting commercial logistics to worker-owned co-ops) and `OpenSourceSeeds`'s **OSSL** (contractually binding derivative seed lines).
5. **Regional Divergence**: In the West, open hardware is an ideological and economic resistance to corporate monopolies; in the Global South, it is a local manufacturing strategy to avoid import tariffs (OpenFlexure in Tanzania/Panama); in China, low-cost commercial manufacturing renders open hardware CAD redundant, while open software functions primarily as system-integrator boilerplate for municipal tenders (`农业岛`).

## Currents (v3)

### Current 1 — Farmer-Maker, Self-Build & De-escalation Hacks
* **Core**: L'Atelier Paysan (SCIC cooperative, liquidated April 2026), AgOpenGPS/Tischler, Farm Hack, Vinduino, Real Seeds DIY Seed Cleaner, ColdSnap/CoolBot controller.
* **v3 Synthesis**: The self-build layer is bifurcated between heavy machinery (which suffers acute cooperative financial fragility) and **low-cost electronic/mechanical retrofits** (`Real Seeds`, `ColdSnap`), which enjoy universal, zero-barrier grassroots replication without institutional overhead.

### Current 2 — State, Multilateral & Supranational Infrastructure
* **Core**: UN FAO Open Foris / SEPAL (180+ countries, MIT), India's AgriStack / Bhashini language DPI, Japan's WAGRI, EU CEADS, China's All Farmers Code (全农码) and National Agricultural Data Centers.
* **v3 Synthesis**: Multilateral UN-funded open software (`SEPAL`, `AquaCrop-OSPy`) represents the most durable, globally deployed open-source public infrastructure, providing zero-cost satellite analytics and crop-water modeling to smallholders worldwide without proprietary SaaS tolls.

### Current 3 — The Open-Core + Paid/Managed Services Hybrid
* **Core**: Software: `farmOS`, `ODK`, `Ekylibre`; Hardware: `FarmBot`, `OpenSprinkler` (hardware sales fund open R&D), `OS Farm`; AI: `FarmerChat` (Digital Green / 1.6M farmers).
* **v3 Synthesis**: Hardware projects with commercial device manufacturing (`OpenSprinkler`, `FarmBot`) demonstrate 10–15+ year financial durability, outlasting purely grant-funded non-profit software projects.

### Current 4 — Commons Governance & Platform Cooperativism
* **Core**: `CoopCycle` (Coopyleft / 70+ worker co-ops), `Open Food Network` (AGPL-3.0 / 20+ countries), `SAOS / ScotEID / Scottish Agriculture Data Partnership`, `JoinData`, `Bioleft`, `Potato Park`.
* **v3 Synthesis**: Platform cooperativism has moved from theory to deployed infrastructure in short-chain distribution. `CoopCycle`'s Coopyleft license demonstrates that software licenses can legally mandate worker/producer ownership, successfully preventing gig-economy platform extraction.

### Current 5 — The Open-Science Diagnostic & Calibration Commons
* **Core**: `Our Sci Reflectometer / Bionutrient Meter` (GPL-3.0), `OpenFlexure Microscope` (CERN-OHL-S-2.0 / GPL-3.0), `PhotosynQ / MultispeQ`.
* **v3 Synthesis**: Open hardware dismantles the $10k–$50k lab instrument barrier. Open science succeeds when hardware schematics are paired with public, crowd-sourced training datasets (`our-sci/bionutrient-institute/dataset`), allowing continuous, transparent recalibration for local soil health and crop nutrition.

### Current 6 — Biological Commons & Agronomic Foundations
* **Core**: `OSSI`, `OpenSourceSeeds (OSSL)`, `Bioleft`, `Potato Park`, `SEARICE`, `Compost for Good`, `Kon-Tiki Kiln`.
* **v3 Synthesis**: The biological commons (seeds, soil microbiology, compost) relies fundamentally on the physical open-hardware layer (`Real Seeds` cleaner, `Kon-Tiki` biochar kiln) to process, clean, and propagate open-source biological assets.

### Current 7 — Interface-Open vs. License-Closed Enclosure
* **Core**: `Priva Connected` / `Hoogendoorn IIVO` (C-OSA-06), `OADA` reference server without adoption (C-OSA-01), `FTC/Deere` repair order (G-OSA-16), `MIT OpenAg` (C-OSA-05).
* **v3 Synthesis**: Corporate incumbents market "open platform architectures" and "open APIs" that remain strictly gated by proprietary subscriptions and approved partner networks. Open APIs do not equal open source.

3. **Does not provide procurement or legal advice**: Open hardware implementations must comply with local electrical, pressure-vessel, and animal-welfare regulations.
