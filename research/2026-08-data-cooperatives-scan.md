# Data cooperatives and farmer data governance beyond the Netherlands: discovery scan

Research date: 2026-08-02
Method: bounded discovery cycle targeting G-OSA-07 / Q-OSA-07 ("Which data cooperatives outside the Netherlands have achieved durable farmer participation and control?"). Six query sets; key sources read in full (NAPDC Data Governance Framework page, Agdatahub enterprise page, Hoosier Ag Today on the GiSC/GADC/ADC merger); ownership and governance verified from primary and analytical sources.

## Why this cell

The collection's farmer-data-governance record was a single case (JoinData, Netherlands). This cycle tests whether the cooperative form exists elsewhere, and distinguishes it from lookalikes: DGA-aligned intermediation companies, donor-funded governance paradigms, and "farmer-owned" branding on venture-backed platforms.

## Currents

### Current 1 — Grower-controlled data cooperatives (US)
The GiSC → GADC/ADC AgXchange → NAPDC lineage: a grower-owned cooperative governed by a board of grower peers, with a neutral storage and sharing repository, now co-creating a farmer-centered data governance framework (NPCC/Portland State; workgroup active since May 2025; NAPDC Ag Data Principles drafted December 2025; UNL-led, NIFA-funded). The US answer to JoinData.

### Current 2 — DGA-aligned data intermediation (EU)
Agdatahub (France): a sector- and institution-owned company acting as a neutral data intermediation service under the EU Data Governance Act, with farmer-centric consent architecture (digital identity, granular authorisation management, revocation), the API-Agro open standard, Gaia-X membership, and a free-for-farmers/B2B-fees business model. The company-form counterpart to the cooperative form.

### Current 3 — The farmer-centric data governance paradigm
Donor- and research-led frameworks: the USAID/Gates "Farmer-Centric Data Governance" report (Development Gateway + Athena Infonomics; six deep dives, nine case studies, incl. a data-cooperatives deep dive); the academic current on cooperative data governance ("100 years of data sovereignty", Hutchins 2023, cited 25; "Data Cooperatives: Democratic Models for Ethical Data Stewardship", 2025); voluntary codes (Australian Farm Data Code by the National Farmers' Federation; Ag Data Transparent certification).

### Current 4 — Contested "farmer-owned" platforms
Farmers Business Network: anonymous data pooling marketed as farmer empowerment, owned by venture capital (US$917.9M raised; Google Ventures, Temasek, T. Rowe Price; IPO trajectory). The boundary case for the whole cell.

## Contested claims

- **FBN is "farmer-owned" / empowers farmers (company framing).** Asserting: FBN marketing and case-study coverage ("farmer-to-farmer network", "farmers frustrated with an unfair ag market"). Contesting: venture ownership (Google Ventures, Temasek; $917.9M), the IPO trajectory, and the live question of data safety if the company goes public. Last reviewed: 2026-08-02.
- **"Farmer-centric" data governance is a settled paradigm (donor reports).** Asserting: the USAID/Gates report and UN Stats blog framing. Contesting: the paradigm is donor-researched and interview-based; it recommends rather than demonstrates durable farmer control. Last reviewed: 2026-08-02.
- **Agdatahub puts "farmers at the centre" (platform and MDPI framing).** Asserting: the MDPI 2026 governance analysis and company framing. Contesting: the centre is consent design, not ownership; shareholders are sector companies and a public bank, not farmers. Last reviewed: 2026-08-02.
- **The cooperative form is the answer to farm data control (advocacy framing).** Asserting: the data-cooperative literature and NAPDC's own framing. Contesting: NAPDC's own governance framework is still co-design in progress, and no located cooperative shows independently verified durable scale — the question G-OSA-07 asks remains partly unanswered. Last reviewed: 2026-08-02.

## Gaps

- **G-DC-01 — Durable farmer participation and control evidence.** Membership scale, platform usage, and control outcomes for NAPDC/GADC and Agdatahub are unverified; SAOS (Scotland) and GISC (per the Policy Review glossary) are leads for a follow-up pass. Registered as G-OSA-20.
- **G-DC-02 — What happens to farmer data when platforms are VC-funded or go public.** The FBN IPO question is live and generalisable; no located governance mechanism addresses it.
- **G-DC-03 — Global South data cooperatives.** None located; the farmer-agency question from G-OSA-18 extends here.

## Actors (critical lens)

- **NAPDC / NPCC / UNL** — the active US cooperative governance work; the strongest "control by design" case.
- **Agdatahub / API-Agro** — sector-owned intermediation; the DGA-aligned counterpart.
- **Development Gateway / Athena Infonomics** — the donor-driven paradigm; treat recommendations as recommendations.
- **Counterweights, named to position:** FBN (venture-owned "farmer" branding), NFF/AU Farm Data Code and Ag Data Transparent (voluntary codes with enforcement limits).

## Freshness model

- NAPDC framework work: `claim` — re-verify annually; active as of research date.
- Agdatahub DGA registration: `fact` — re-verify annually.

- Cooperative scale evidence: `gap` — actively sought, not verified.

## What this scan does not yet do

- **No Global South pass.** Data cooperatives in Africa, Asia, and Latin America were not searched beyond the general query (G-DC-03).
- **No member-level verification.** No interviews or member-level evidence for NAPDC or Agdatahub; the records rest on project and analytical sources.
- **SAOS and GISC not fully read.** Both surfaced as leads from the Policy Review glossary and warrant a follow-up.
- **No audit of FBN's actual data terms.** The candidate record relies on framing-level evidence.
